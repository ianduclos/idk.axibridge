The correction

Ian asked for tighter, more stylized curves and permission for another regime to take over Territory. His sketch was illustrative; the earlier Territory image and the paintings supplied relationships and intricacy, not templates. The first drafts nevertheless used preset folds and pinches. His criticism that the curves were being followed literally was correct.

The next construction used contacts, pressure and exchanges of actual course continuations. It changed the network but its elastic relaxation and short cubic controls still softened the turns. Ian's further correction—“not enough bulbs or corners”—changed the fitting stage directly. Inflation now follows substantial bends found in the generated courses, adjacent spans have unequal control reaches, selected incoming handles collapse to 4.5% of their span, and entry/exit tangents can disagree. No reference-image tracing or fixed tuple of curve nodes is used. These remain aesthetic biases written into geometry; a more elaborate mechanism alone is not an artistic result.

A trial with enlarged controls everywhere produced rows of teeth and spikes. That was discarded. Current inflation is localized to separated bends and joins. The failed literal, angular, softened and overinflated trials are retained in tools/territory02/takeover/trial1 through trial5.

What is visibly different

The primary inspected both whole populations and matched close views. In current seed 47, the doubled upper course makes an abrupt elbow, swells into a round lower passage and returns through a narrow crossing. Its companions stop at unequal points. It offers a stronger bulb/corner contrast than the previous fit, although the whole drawing still scatters its attention.

13 develops a tall narrow lobe next to a short returning hook. Those two forms have different proportions and release into long diagonal passages. The current version also loses some of the previous fit's concentrated central entanglement. It is not simply more intricate everywhere.

82 gives the upper-left passage a larger rounded interior, a corner on its approach and a small junction where companions depart from the main course. The large lower-right return remains quite gentle. 18 has a clearer short neck between a bulb and a diagonal reach, alongside detached marks whose role is uncertain. 5 and 24 retain long quiet stretches; the local treatment does not yet change the authority of every course.

The new source was frozen before first rendering 107, 173, 227, 281. These are fresh screen evidence; no tuning followed their inspection. 107 spreads small lobes across long crossing directions. 227 concentrates a doubled compressed return near the upper middle. 173 and especially 281 remain sparse and loosely related as wholes. That limits any claim that the new fit reliably delivers intricate construction. Earlier holdouts 31/69/103/151 belonged to the previous fit and were seen before this change; they are archived, not reported as fresh current evidence.

Independent review and retained disagreement

A fresh bounded Sol reviewer first inspected neutral populations and removal counterfactuals, then the current neutral population, before seeing mappings, mechanisms, Ian's corrections or the lead's preferences. Its image account notices actual current bulb/corner changes in 47/107/227, but distinguishes that from reliable intricacy. It also preserves the loss of earlier entanglement in 13 and the weak fresh 173/281 cases. It preferred a trial4 variant with one path removed while finding a different two-path removal damaging; omission was consequential, not a general instruction to simplify.

Sol finds current47 the strongest new argument for a course governing a large blank interior. The primary finds its local bulb/corner contrast useful while remaining less convinced by the whole sheet's scattered attention. Both views are retained. An explicit curve bias can still produce a recognizable device: absence of a fixed coordinate library does not establish aesthetic independence or variety. The review is evidence for discussion, not automatic acceptance.

Scope and verification

Stage 0 shows the ordinary sparse Territory rendering. The first rewrite uses its latent migration centrelines, bypassing the usual hand and bundles, rather than editing every emitted stroke. The second rewrite consumes the first pass's actual returned course geometry; it can exchange continuations and leave cuts. Short restatement strands are rebuilt, not fed back as extra courses. All visible paths use 0.1 mm line width; density is zero. No filled or opacity-based weight was added.

Strategies to discuss after looking

  1. Keep Territory's broad courses and apply the new inflation/corner rule only to selected interactions. This may preserve useful empty interiors but may leave the overall result too familiar.
  2. Let the replacement own the course network early, including continuations and cuts. This gives more freedom and can also dismantle promising relations.
  3. Treat both intermediate and takeover outputs as elements for composition, rather than always keeping the last stage. This preserves losses and alternate directions at the cost of making selection part of the working process.

These are proposals for Ian's response. None has been integrated or chosen as a new default. The earlier report's three-maker convergence proposals remain available alongside this additional path.
