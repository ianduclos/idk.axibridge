# Corner second-eye

This is a disclosed-mechanism screen review. J4 preserves a useful corner at normal width. The inner edge keeps a crisp point while the outer strands turn through a smooth, nested bowl. One side articulates the corner; the other carries its width. Spacing is much calmer than J3, with no visible local loops or crossings. The expression changes from a full chevron to a sharp inner turn within a rounded outer mass; given the stated priority, that feels coherent.

I do not see a dominant remaining defect in J4. The innermost strands compress tightly around the point and become difficult to follow at contact-sheet scale, but do not form a visible knot. The preview's three modes retain the same legible relation.

J6 remains an extreme warning. Several upper strands appear coincident or nearly coincident through the center, then separate into a shelf and kink; spacing changes abruptly where it rejoins the profile. This looks mechanically constrained and is not evidence of even spacing at extreme width.

Paper behavior and user acceptance remain provisional.
