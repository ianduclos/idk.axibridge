## After the mechanism reveal

The reveal changes the causal interpretation, not the preferences. **R03** and **R08** are ungrouped new outputs, yet both produced strong relations among unequal crests; they are good reasons to preserve the ungrouped setting as a distinct source rather than treat grouping as a correction. Conversely, phrasing is not an aesthetic guarantee: **R04** benefits from its delayed late cluster, while **R01, R09, and R10** are quieter or more bead-like under the same phrasing level. The fully regular identity of **R12** confirms the repetitive pole seen in the blind pass.

On curves, **R14** shows grouping and path change reinforcing each other, but **R13** shows that the same family can still leave the lobes largely interchangeable. **R15** remains hard to attribute because overlap and its enclosed void dominate the crest hierarchy. I would therefore retain both grouped and ungrouped sources and judge phrasing by whether it produces a visible relation in a given result, not by the setting alone.
