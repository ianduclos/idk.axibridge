# Territory v2 blind review (Sonnet reviewer, 2026-09-29)

Reviewer: Sonnet 5.5 (assigned by Ian, overriding the skill's Sol default). Screen previews only; nothing here is a paper judgement. Sections 1-2 were recorded before opening recipes.json.

## 1. Image-only reading (blind)

Observations
- L: closed, smooth, self-contained blobs, capsules and spirals scattered on empty ground. Uniform finish, uniform scale, large even gaps. Reads as an organism/pebble catalogue, the "blob production" failure. Spiral curls recur in almost every cell (L1, L3, L5, L6, L8, L9, L10, L11). Same handwriting everywhere.
- K: lines break into dashes and open contours. Neighbouring shapes share edges (K3, K12 centre, K10 upper band), so shape reads through the gaps between marks. Wobbly, pressed-against-each-other territories, with a few tight curls (K7 top-left, K3 hook). Very few closed forms. Fragmentation is fairly uniform in density (roughly the same dash length everywhere) but the shapes and clustering vary a lot cell to cell.
- M: K plus extra straight-ish internal lines. Same skeleton as K on the same cells, so difference is the added strokes: M5 long diagonal spine and a horizontal slash left; M2 ladder-like straights inside the centre mass; M4 a squiggle inside the right territory; M3 a bottom-right cross-line closing the lower-right shape. These straight interior lines have different authority from the wobbly contours: they read as construction/scaffolding across a form.
- N: nearly empty. N1, N2, N3, N4, N5, N10, N12 hold a few sparse fragments; N6, N7, N8, N9, N11 are one or two marks. Interior lines and borders only. Weight reads as thin gesture fragments. Quiet, but most cells offer nothing to follow.
- O: nearly identical to K on most cells (O2, O3, O4, O5, O6, O7, O8, O10 read the same as K). Differences from K: O9 and O11/O12 lose or change some shapes (O11 loses K11's round upper shapes and gains a knotted blob top-left; O9 gets a hooked line bottom-left and loses K9's top-left dashes).

Interpretations (tentative)
- K/O are the most alive: dashed contours make the eye complete forms, so empty space between fragments takes shape (K3 centre, K12 wedge between shapes, K10). Passages carry unequal authority: some closed loops, some open dashes.
- Cells to keep: K3/O3 (crowded centre against open right; tiny hook at right as a stray), K7/O7 (spiral top-left vs. dashed long diagonal vs. bow-tie blob top-right, three grammars in one), K12/O12, K10/O10 (banded upper zone against sparse lower), K5/O5 (large single mass pressing right, empty left half), K1/O1.
- Weak/ordinary: K9/O9 (drifts to bottom band, sparse), K8/O8 (scattered small shapes, close to L's blob catalogue), K6.
- Defects to protect: broken dashes with gaps, hooks/curls appearing rarely (K3 hook, K7 spiral), overlaps of near-parallel lines at borders (K3 right, K4 centre). Defect that flattens: the constant dash length and near-constant line character across a cell (some "one handwriting"), and in K8/K11 a fairly regular ring of separate lumps.
- Contour-map look: mild in K3/K4/K10 where nested wobbly loops sit side by side; not dominant.

## 2. After reading recipes.json

Recipes: K = borders+outer; O = K + voids; M = O + interior; N = borders + voids + interior, no outer. Everything else equal.

Observations/effect on judgement
- The recipe explains N: dropping outer edges removes almost everything, confirming outer edges carry the drawing's mass. N is not a lower-density variant of the same idea; it is a different, mostly empty one.
- O vs K share nearly all cells because voids rarely bind. Where they do (O9, O11, O12) the change is visible but small, and not clearly better or worse; O11 gets a more tangled top-left mass while losing the K11 octagon.
- M's difference is only interior marks. That matches my read: the added spines are the only straight/authoritative-different lines on any sheet. Judgement unchanged, but I now attribute M5's diagonal and M3's cross-line to "spine/echo/crossing" marks and would test those separately.
- Voids changing the drawing while claiming to be invisible negative space is the only place empty space is actively shaped by the process. I could not see that as shaped emptiness in most cells; it only shows as omitted shapes.

## 3. Recommendations

1. Develop the outer+borders base (K/O) further; it is the only family whose fragments make the eye do work. Drop N as a candidate in its current form (N6-N9, N11 are inert).
2. Prefer O over K only if voids can be shown to matter: on O9, O11, O12 the differences are marginal. Run voids with a stronger effect on a few cells before adopting them.
3. Keep interior marks (M) but reduce them to one per drawing at most: M5's long diagonal and M3's bottom-right cross-line add a different authority; M2's ladder and M4's squiggle look like scribble scaffolding and flatten.
4. Vary dash length and gap by territory (K3, K10, K12): now near-constant; a few long unbroken passages against heavily broken ones would give unequal authority without new marks.
5. Protect the rare hooks and curls (K3 right hook, K7 top-left spiral, K1 spiral); do not let them become frequent as in L.
6. Guard against the lump-ring look in K8/K11/O8: shapes similar in size, evenly spaced. A stronger size or density difference between territories would help.
7. Keep L only as a control; do not port its closed-blob output.
8. Next test: same seed, K base, interior limited to a single crossing line, plus a variant with region-dependent fragmentation; compare on cells 3, 5, 7, 12.

Limits: screen only; only cells 3 and 7 seen at close view; the sheets do not show paper line weight.
