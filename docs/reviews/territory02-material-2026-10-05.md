# Territory02: material trials and the grammar objection

The three requested experiments are available separately in the
[material comparison](../../tools/territory02/takeover/material/index.html).
They produce some consequential local changes, but do not solve the limited
vocabulary Ian identified during the run. The subsequent
[grammar comparison](../../tools/territory02/takeover/grammar/index.html)
removes inherited ornaments and constructs genuinely different paths. That
probe remains loop-heavy and uneven. Neither study is a selected replacement,
and the three material mechanisms have not been converged.

## What the three experiments actually changed

1. **Opening** gathers real grown strands into local bodies within otherwise
   bare courses. The first version compressed donors into repeated black
   spindles. A bounded revision widened unequal flanks, thinned whole donors
   and staggered release. Opening13 now has more open internal structure, but
   its angular outer strands can look grafted on. Opening47 and fresh521 are
   often faint annotations. A causal origin is not enough: several bodies
   barely change the surrounding emptiness. The isolated event close-up of13
   exposes the projection's angularity more clearly than its whole sheet.
2. **Pressure** uses measured nearby ink to move a course, then transfers some
   displacement into sparse texture. In5 the changed lower junction creates
   a useful hinge; in587 several passages negotiate the central knot more
   visibly. The inherited material remains recognizably coarse. Pressure24
   also shows the limit: thin displaced curves, comb-like edges and a still
   prominent star remain together without acquiring a convincing shared
   purpose. Some repeated serration is a new mannerism, not broader grammar.
3. **Unravelling** extracts an actual grown run, redistributes neighbouring
   grain to a torn rim and carries a few scraps with it. In5 the pale angular
   opening visibly interrupts a previously fluent diagonal bank; in587 the
   torn rim alters the authority of the right-hand mass. The exposed strands
   themselves do less than the concept promised. Removing them from5 leaves
   much of the effect intact. Some cavities look cut out with a fairly uniform
   width, despite irregular boundaries. That is a real weakness to retain.

The independent reviewer read neutral H/J/P calibration before the mechanism
reveal. It noticed the cavity's consequence and the weaker difference between
keeping and removing exposed pulls. Across the population it protected F-5's
hinge and F/R-587's changed junctions, while finding detached L fragments and
recurring F serration. Its [report](territory02-material-sol.md) preserves the
initial and contextual readings. Ian's grammar complaint arrived between the
calibration and population reading; the latter was identity-blind but not
preference-blind.

I agree with the review's central criticism. I would retain the awkward open
body in unravelling5 as useful material even though it looks cut rather than
naturally grown. I would not require every scrap to justify itself, but the
H/P comparison prevents presenting their provenance as the main visual gain.
Pressure can be subtler at full-sheet scale than its causal account suggests.

## Why the same devices kept coming back

The old density code explicitly constructs a radial thorn with 5–11 spokes.
Its slash routine also supplies repeated chords and occasional crossed Xs.
The takeover fitter repeatedly applies the same 3.5 perpendicular inflation
and compressed incoming handle, followed by local restatements. All three
material experiments retained that vocabulary as their starting point.
Changing what happens around it was too narrow a response to the desired
radical change. Ian's objection changed the direction of the work.

The separate grammar probe generates courses through unequal travel and turning
intervals, with independently varying turn concentration, scale and occasional
corners. Earlier courses can locally redirect a new passage. Source positions
and initial directions remain connected to Territory; the new paths do not
retain its fitted skeleton. The proposed general cut/join surgery is not yet
implemented: this trial replaces the path constructor instead.

Two failed development versions are retained. The first was loose and cursive;
the second overused tiny coils. The current version gives compact turns more
length, producing larger bodies and return paths. In13, interleaved enclosures
and a cornered return differ visibly from the earlier doubled bulb. In24 the
long upright and clustered lower turns redistribute the drawing. Fresh739 and
797 have different open enclosures; 853 packs too many related loops into one
cluster, while911 leaves a narrow stack of gestures. The new population still
speaks in loops and smooth hooks. This is a changed vocabulary with its own
restriction, not evidence of open-ended invention.

The stripped recipe disables continue/slash/surprise at generation and retains
the actual coarse grow/finish machinery. It also sets planes 0, cover 0.32 and
nominal ink 24 m. The earlier full reference remains nominal 60 m. An extra control
applies the same stripped recipe to earlier curves. These comparisons separate
new paths from the stripped recipe, but **do not isolate star removal alone**:
several recipe settings change together. Texture24 gains a large upper mass;
texture797 makes an enclosing bank on the left. Elsewhere it still becomes
familiar contour waves or competes with a delicate bare passage. I would keep
bare and textured versions visible rather than treating texture as completion.

The second-eye addendum supports a visible change in continuity, particularly
the compound banks in fresh797, while also finding repeated soft hooks and
contour tufts. I retain the narrower conclusion: different paths are visible,
but broader invention and better whole-sheet coherence remain unproven.

## Possible convergence strategies — not implemented

A. **Change the grammar first.** Work from the new bare-path population and
   selected earlier bare passages, preserving counterexamples. Delay combining
   material mechanisms until the lines themselves offer enough differences.
   This most directly addresses the latest objection; it risks losing the
   earlier takeover's sharper, more peculiar local character.

B. **Use one material event as a structural edit.** Let an opening or tear
   remove, redirect or split the course that generated it, then continue from
   the changed drawing. The cavity could alter subsequent structure instead
   of becoming a recurring finish. The risk is another compulsory “interesting”
   event, especially if each sheet receives the same number of interventions.

C. **Compose from selected fragments.** Keep strong bare passages, grain and
   occasional torn bodies as separate elements for human selection and
   placement. This may use the current material better than forcing a single
   generator to resolve every sheet. It shifts more authorship to composition.

These are options for Ian's response, not a chosen architecture or merge plan.

## Evidence and verification

source freezes, whole sheets, matched details, actual ink/hashes and failed
source versions. Material fresh seeds are 521/587/631/683; grammar fresh seeds
are 739/797/853/911. No mechanism tuning followed either fresh population.

Each viewer has 50 geometry cases and 100 whole/detail image states: 100 cases
and 200 images total. Repeatability, input purity, finite bounded geometry and
physical ceilings passed. Source hashes still match the freezes. Original
baseline parity was rechecked separately. Stroke width remains 0.1 mm.
Material ink ranges are 1.56–2.96m opening, 10.85–33.09m pressure and 10.39–32.72m
unravelling. New bare paths use 0.92–2.12m; their textured versions 6.48–17.59m.
These describe physical marks, not aesthetic merit.
