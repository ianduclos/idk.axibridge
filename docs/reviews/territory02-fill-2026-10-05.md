## What changed visibly

Shared bands draw through the interval between actual rewritten courses.
Unequal curved strokes crowd into dark wedges, lenses and divided fans.
Shared pockets hatch the same selected regions with parallel straight lines,
making flatter, generally paler planes. These marks use the requested thin
0.1 mm stroke: their weight comes from physical line fields.

Seed 13 is the clearest gain. The bands give the left diagonal, central lens
and small right curl a common passage. White channels keep adjacent masses
apart and let the thin course alternate between boundary and crossing. The
pockets preserve that construction but give its parts a quieter, more planar
authority. Neither is simply the original curve made thicker.

Seed 47 is less convincing as a whole. Several filled petals make the lower
course richer, but widely spaced parts remain uninvolved. In 82, the dark
left passage makes the upper loop and lower sweep answer one another;
another small patch can feel like a separate badge. Straight span end caps
sometimes impose a triangle that the drawing has not otherwise earned.

Fresh 113 makes a long horizontal encounter carry several smaller turns.
Fresh 179 gains dark prongs around the upper/right courses while the large
empty middle remains weakly articulated. Fresh 233 has strong local wedges
at separated incidents; they do not necessarily change the relation between
those incidents. Fresh 293 gains a central construction, but its broad right
arc remains largely detached. Extra material is therefore consequential,
without establishing reliable whole-sheet coherence.

## Limits and possible next tests

Only curves-alone/bands/pockets isolate fill changes: each retains identical
base strokes. Ordinary filled Territory uses different course geometry and
serves as a reference for its distributed bundles and fading authority.
Light/dense are 8/18 m nominal allowances, not forced density levels. Small
regions can saturate the 0.2 mm minimum pitch; original Territory can slightly
exceed its nominal allowance when ordering joins short gaps. Actual ink is
recorded. Screen crowding and moiré are not evidence of paper tone.

One unselected strategy is to test connected region selection in 47 or 233,
where a selected pair is detached from the others, while controlling total
ink and region count. Connectivity cannot be the sole criterion: 13 and 179
already share a principal course across all three regions and differ visibly
in coherence. Another strategy is to change how material enters and leaves
each passage, replacing arbitrary straight span caps with a taper responding
to the courses. A third is to let one strong filled encounter reshape or
suppress neighbouring curves, testing whether line and fill can govern each
other. Those are proposals for Ian's response; none has been silently merged.
