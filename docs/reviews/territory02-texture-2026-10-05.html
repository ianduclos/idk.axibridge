Ian rejected the preceding panel fills as worse than the earlier baseline. He values that baseline's texture, sense of material and integration with the lines, and wants the integration to go further. The flat-fill trials are now explicitly archived as rejected. This follow-up returns to the actual previous texture generator and full recipe, rather than inventing another shading vocabulary. Comparison and construction/reproduction.

What is restored

The earlier texture gets its variation through inherited line families: successive echoes distort, pinch, spread, break, retrace and acquire dry skips. Those qualities were lost in my bounded patch construction. The previous fill trial also reduced nominal ink from the full baseline's 60 m to 8/18 m; its comparison was therefore not a faithful continuation of the full baseline. The earlier report's local gains do not override Ian's rejection.

Here the original full Territory output is verified identical against its unmodified engine at every seed. Two probes apply that same density/texture machinery to the unchanged takeover curves. One retains original activity and history guidance. The other rebuilds those guides around actual first- and second-pass courses and their geometric change. Present geometry, tangents, junctions and occupancy follow the new curves in both probes. Both retain the original finish and full recipe. No new default or production convergence has been selected.

Primary visual reading

The material quality returns substantially: the new sheets have feathered, uneven families, pinched grain and doubled-back marks rather than dark panels. That is recovery toward the baseline, not proof that either new population surpasses it. The original remains particularly valuable for how a family grows and fades along a course and how sparse crossings counter its weight.

Seed 13's inherited-guidance version produces a divided, curving passage in the lower half. Rebuilt guidance moves much of that authority toward the left and leaves another long weighted course on the right. Both recover texture, but the tight central curves can become minor beside those larger families. In 24, inherited guidance creates a large left fan that feels less particular than the original's quieter diagonal construction; rebuilt guidance leaves more of the blank field available. In 47, rebuilt guidance spreads large families across the right half. Their richness does not settle whether they belong to the important new-line encounter.

Fresh 331 is an explicit weak case for integration: a major family appears in the left field, away from the bare drawing's strongest right-side activity. Fresh 389 recovers varied grain and crossings, but the automatically selected texture detail contains no bare principal course. The density machinery can seed from its own secondary continuation/ribbon strokes, allowing the texture to develop elsewhere. Fresh 419 and 463 recover useful interrupted families around some bends, while also adding accents that compete with the thin line construction. Whole-sheet authority remains uneven.

Independent reading and retained disagreement

Sol first saw neutral populations and whole/detail removals before receiving identities or preferences. It found texture consequential in 419, 331 and the original 13, and valued the quiet rebuilt-guidance 24. It also found broad coverage liable to turn curves into supports for familiar ornamental bundles. Its suggested test holds placement at two 419 encounters while varying local interruption, unequal retracing and removal.

I agree that those local endings and interruptions carry much of the material interest. I challenged 331 as evidence of integration: the strong left family is remote from the bare drawing's main right-side activity. After context, Sol retained its asymmetry reading but withdrew that lobe as evidence of integration. These are distinct claims; the initial account remains recorded. I also regard recurring grain as a legitimate part of Ian's established baseline. Its familiarity alone is not a reason to replace it; the problem is whether its placement and weight make the encountered curves more consequential. No reviewer preference is automatic acceptance.

What this test can establish

The bare and two new textured versions have byte-identical base strokes; their differences isolate texture addition and its guidance. Original full Territory has different courses and remains the artistic baseline, not a fill-only before/after control. All use the same nominal 60 m recipe, but actual ink differs: original 11.9–32.8 m, inherited guidance 11.4–33.1 m, rebuilt guidance 10.2–32.4 m across these ten seeds. More marks are not a score.

Source was frozen before fresh 331/389/419/463; no texture or curve tuning followed those images. All 40 cases passed original baseline parity, repeatability, input/base purity, finite geometry, bed and physical limits. The 80 whole/detail viewer images and controls/layout passed in-memory checks using own SVG/CSS text, with no URL navigation or external requests. Actual in-app local-file navigation remains unverified after the earlier automatic security rejection. Everything is a screen probe; paper and interactive drawing acceptance remain open.

The matched details deliberately frame a densely occupied grown-ink tile. They can miss a better baseline passage or the central new-line encounter. Fine strokes can merge at thumbnail size. Whole populations and removal calibration remain necessary evidence, not selected detail highlights.

Unselected continuation strategies

The next narrow test could retain this baseline texture but allow growth seeds only on principal rewritten courses, separating integration from the extra material generated by secondary continuations. That could also remove valuable counterweights, so it should be compared rather than assumed better. Another path would let an established family alter the course that produced it, making line and texture negotiate through a second pass. A more restrained path would preserve the full baseline's distribution and apply tighter turns only where its existing families have authority. These are proposals for Ian's response, not a convergence chosen on his behalf.

The bounded blind second-eye reading is retained separately in the Sol report. Primary acceptance remains based on the drawings and Ian's direction, not agreement with that report.
