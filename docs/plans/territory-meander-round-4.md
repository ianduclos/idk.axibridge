## Direction

The plan is one **Density** dial (0–1). At 0 it is exactly Version 7. It has four sub-dials, and all of them are in:
1. **Continue:** shapes carry on; bands run past their ends and wrap; long ribbons cross the sheet.
2. **Cover:** fields are covered with flow-following streaks, fans inside lobes, and voids kept as negative shapes.
3. **Slash:** straight cuts and shard wedges.
4. **Surprise:** one region per sheet in another register.

It should be "a bit smart and contextually aware, but not conservative". An **ink limit** dial runs up to 100 m (default about 40 m), with continuous strokes preferred to keep pen lifts and plot time down. Overlap is fine with his thin pen.

## Constraints and standing taste

- One pen, no colour, no `axibridge/` changes, no plotting, screen only.
- Standing rejects (`docs/plans/territory-meander-round.md`) still hold:
  - short straight hatch dashes;
  - engraving, weather-chart or topographic looks;
  - scribble-filter restatement of every edge;
  - hairball clusters;
  - blobs and beans.
- **Cover** must read as painterly streak and fan, not as hatching.
- The loop is the same as before: an Opus brainstorm, a Fable brief, a checkpointed build, at most 2 blind Sonnet reviews, then publish Version 8 to the same link.
