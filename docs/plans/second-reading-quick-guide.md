# Second Reading — quick guide

You and the machine take turns adding to one drawing. It can extend a line,
borrow a motif or connect existing passages. It may answer older marks instead
of your newest stroke. Continue adds a machine turn; Your turn lets you draw;
Try another keeps this version and offers a different continuation.

- **Attention:** nearby/current material → wider drawing, including older marks.
- **Departure:** a close response → a more transformed response.
- **Scale:** shorter extensions/narrower connections → longer/broader ones.
  Copied motifs keep their relationship to the original size.
- **Pen smoothing:** removes hand wobble while retaining deliberate corners;
  applies to your next stroke, not strokes already recorded.
- **Reading:** selects the response policy. Responsive is the normal starting
  point. Older policies expose Persistence (how long it pursues something),
  Reach (how far it extends or shifts) and Recurrence (how often it returns).
- **Turn:** inspect an earlier stage. Revising it preserves the other future.
- **Seed:** the starting variation. Randomize picks a new seed; New drawing
  applies it, along with width, height and boundary mode.

**Boundaries:** Clip cuts machine lines at the sheet edge. Turn inside bends
machine excursions back into the sheet. Overshoot + fit allows drawing beyond
the dashed sheet and fits the whole element together when kept. The surrounding
work surface stays fixed. Select a boundary and press **New drawing** to use it;
the bench states which mode is active.

Keep makes an ordinary layer. Resume continues its recorded drawing without
changing the existing layer. Thick reinforcement stacks are removed from normal
use; archived study replay preserves the original evidence separately.
