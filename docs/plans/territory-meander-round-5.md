- Option 1 was "Lines become the fill": strands peel off the bands and multiply into fields, as echoes, fans and continuations. Standalone planes stay rare, and appear only where they cross a line or as an isolated event.
- Option 3 was "Both, with a dial between": a sub-dial runs from "lines make the field" to "fields meet the lines".
- The river image is the Rio Mamoré (`docs/research/meander5/ref/river-mamore.jpg`, also used in round 1). Its scroll bars, oxbows and meander scars are field made of the line's own history. Where the pattern changes along the way, those are the "mutations within".

He chose to run it in this session.

## Direction

1. **Line-grown field (the new default).** Coverage is made from the drawing's own strands:
   - offset echoes and scroll bars that migrate away from a band;
   - peel-offs that leave a band and fill a lobe;
   - fanned repeats.
   Each is one continuous pen line where possible.
2. **Mutations within.** An echo train changes along its run:
   - its spacing jumps;
   - one echo buckles into its own small meander;
   - a cutoff closes to an oxbow;
   - a train splits or merges;
   - the direction flips at a scar.
3. **Planes become events.** The Version 8 hatch planes survive only where they cross a line or as an isolated event. A new sub-dial, **Source**, runs from line-grown (0) to Version 8 planes (1).
4. **Cover** defaults to 0.45, as Ian asked.
5. **Push the maxed dials further.** Continue, Slash, Surprise and Density get headroom above 1, marked on the page as "over".
6. Density 0 stays byte-identical to Version 8. Old recipes stay reproducible, with Source defaulting to 0 for new recipes only.

## Constraints

These are the same as in round 4:
- one pen, no colour, no `axibridge/` changes, screen only;
- an ink limit of at most 100 m;
- the standing rejects apply, especially tree rings (evenly spaced concentric arcs everywhere) and topographic contours;
- the loop: an Opus brainstorm, a Fable brief, a build, at most 2 blind Sonnet reviews, then Version 9 at the same link.
