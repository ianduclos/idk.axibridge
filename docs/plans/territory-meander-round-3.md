## What his blue curves do (`ian-bridges.png`)

1. **Fillet at a V** (lower left): a small curve that joins two lines where they meet at a cusp.
2. **Peel-off echo:** a curve that runs parallel just inside an existing line, then departs and lands tangentially on another line.
3. **Long sweep:** it leaves one line's end, crosses the lower band, and lands tangentially on the horizontal line further along. It is the longest.
4. **Continuation S:** it leaves the horizontal line, bends down, and becomes the vertical line's tail. It joins two lines that never met.
5. **Cups:** small arcs hugging a crossing, just under the junction.
6. **A short arc** that crosses a band edge near the tangle.

None of them closes a shape. They sit at junctions and line ends, and each one is smooth and deliberate, calmer than the lines around it.

## Directions

These are D1–D5 in the approved plan (`~/.claude/plans/lucky-doodling-lemur.md`). In short:
- **D1:** a chaos budget, with a calmer hand outside 1–2 chaos zones.
- **D2:** a stylised accent register (the "eyebrows", as in seed 22).
- **D3:** liminal bridges at crossings, near-contacts and line ends, with parallel echoes that resolve at different points.
- **D4:** composition: a second trunk that crosses the first, a 60–100 mm tangle field, and bean and nested-arc guards; the core system may change.
- **D5:** a meander-only page with about 7 controls, randomize-all with locks, copy settings, cores hidden by default, a 12-seed contact sheet, and the old controls deleted.

Not in scope, unchanged: `axibridge/`, plotting, colour or multi-pen, and the bench port.
