## The bar (from round 15's notes)

- **Awareness without it being obvious.** Lines that know about each other
  (sympathy, weave, imitate) at the subtlety of the sympathy close-up. A
  reader should sense it before naming it.
- **Variety inside a technique.** A behaviour that does the same thing every
  time is a template. Crossings, sympathy and devices should each have more
  than one outcome, drawn unpredictably.
- **Hands kept different.** The level-of-detail filter that made the zoom
  work smoothed the hands toward each other. Difference is the point.
- **Strong moves at full strength.** The lead's error in round 15 was
  caution: it hedged on the bold swell and called the ledger "dry". Show the
  strong versions as candidates; don't stretch an old note into a rule.
- **Middle density, elegance, direction** (round 14) still hold.
- **Build for the bench.** Everything new is written against the score v2
  contract (S0 below), so the round's awareness work becomes the synthesis
  bench's first relations, not another composer's private code.

## Phase 0: the contract (lead, before any thread starts)

The design doc's S0, kept small:
- `kit/_synth.py`: score v2 (layers, relations, devices, decisions), the
  executor (layers in order; grow relations, then contact relations; devices
  last), the played log, per-layer cache keys. `synth.run(score)` returns an
  ordinary `Sheet` whose `meta["notes"]["score"]` is the played score, so
  `kit.preview`, `apply_devices` and the existing devices all keep working.
- `kit/_relations.py`: the `@relation(id, phase="grow"|"contact", params=…)`
  registry, `rctx.rng(...)` per relation and event, and the event format.
- Two reference relations so the contract is tested end to end: `cross`
  (no awareness: lines just cross, the control) and `yield` (ported as-is from
  listen).
- Tests: determinism across processes, the log is complete, editing one
  relation re-rolls only it, a v1 score still reads.

Threads may only add relations, devices and experiments in their own files;
the contract is lead-owned (§7: contracts before parallel work).

## The four threads

### A. Awareness as a composable relation (first)

Two parts, one agent:
- **Crossings that vary.** One `crossing` relation whose outcome is drawn per
  crossing from weights: this line breaks (passes under), the other breaks
  (over), both run through, one stops at the other, or the near pass bends
  with the far one. A `streak` param lets an outcome repeat before it
  changes, so it reads as behaviour, not noise. Ian: "not always make gaps …
  above and below unpredictably".
- **Sympathy between any layers.** Lift `aware_marks`' sympathy out of lcorp
  into a relation over any src and dst: the dst takes on the src's twist where
  it runs over or along it, faded by distance. Depth is a param, not the zoom
  channel.
- **Other ideas** ("perhaps there are other ideas"): the agent proposes and
  builds 2–3 awareness relations of its own in the same register (subtle,
  felt before named). Candidates to start from, not to follow: a line that
  slows (denser turning) as it nears another; a pass that leaves room for a
  line not yet drawn; two lines that keep a constant distance for a while,
  then let go; a line that inherits the other's pen weight where they touch.

Images: a population (8 consecutive seeds) of listen sheets re-run through
the executor with the new crossing and sympathy, against the round-15
originals; the same sheets with awareness only (no grow relations); one
close-up per new relation; the 2–3 strongest.

### B. Distinct hands

lcorp's specimen showed 33 of 44 hands yielding by the measure, but by eye
"fine fuzz + one or two big bends". Find what made them converge and undo it:
- level of detail per hand (σ from each hand's own scale ladder, not one
  camera resolution), or no smoothing at all for the hands that don't need it;
- the far-scale calibration (every hand starts at the river's density, which
  may itself be the averaging);
- show each hand at the zoom band where it is most unlike the library
  (measured against the whole library with the 74-feature panel, §7 lesson).

Images: specimen v2 beside v1 (same hands, same layout); a plate of the 8
most distinct hands; one relay or composition that uses the difference.
Risk: "more different" must not become "exaggerated toward a caricature";
grammars stay whole.

### C. Archaeology further

Round 15 revived four families from rounds 8–11. Go further back and wider:
- rounds 2–7: the grammar versions, topology, restyle, tremor, the
  broad-nib model, wet edges, signs as line events;
- the inventory's never-reviewed techniques: coil, restate, river_loops,
  measure_answer, measure_next_rehand, dev_rare_machine, poly_scatter.

Each enters a composition in a role it never had, ideally as a layer or a
relation in a v2 score (a restatement as a relation; tremor as a response to
proximity; wet edges where two layers touch). The agent picks and may drop
what fights back. Images: one piece per revival that holds up, plus what
surprised it; at most 8.

### D. More device documents

Process documents that read the played score, each one file, all optional,
each its own layer:
- a **crossing census**: where every crossing went, and which way (under,
  over, through, stop, sympathy), as a map or a table;
- a **sympathy trace**: the displacement each line took from another, drawn
  beside it;
- a **relation ledger**: the played log as a document (who heard whom, how
  much, where), the round-15 ledger's sibling;
- a **provenance tree**: which hand, corpus and source every line descends
  from;
- the agent's own: 1–2 documents of a process only this bench has.

Images: the documents on thread A's sheets and on two round-15 keepers
(listen 15102, arch 15010), one or two per drawing (round-15 note: more is
"too much apparatus").

## Risks

- **Awareness gets loud.** Varied crossings could read as broken lines
  everywhere. The test is the sympathy close-up's subtlety; the agent shows
  the weights it tried, including too much.
- **The contract is wrong.** Thread A is its first real user. If a relation
  can't be written against it, A stops and reports instead of working around
  it; the lead fixes the contract.
- **Distinct becomes caricature** (B). Measured against the library and
  checked by eye.
- **Archaeology as nostalgia** (C). A revival must take a new role.
- **Devices as decoration** (D). Each must read something true; one or two
  per drawing.
