  composition's result carries a **score**, a JSON list of layers and of the
  relations that fired between them (who listened to whom, how, where). That
  is the substructure he will edit later.

## The four threads

### A. Listening: a jump in composition

Build on `ccolour` (the keeper), not a fifth composer. The new material is a
**vocabulary of relations between layers**, each a mechanism that takes
another layer's line as its input:
- imitate: take its gesture;
- exaggerate: the same gesture, pushed (amplitude, speed, scale);
- translate: the same move, in another line style or hand;
- echo later: the same gesture, elsewhere on the sheet, after a delay;
- refuse: visibly avoid it;
- mask: the 4507 move, a partial void that some layers respect and others cross;
- the ones Ian "isn't thinking about": the agent proposes 2–3 of its own.

Overlaps follow the awareness rule above. Middle density by default; the
density extremes are off. Each sheet writes its score.
Images: a population (8+ consecutive seeds), its 2–3 strongest, and one sheet
annotated with the relations it used, from its score.

### B. Line corpora as compositional technique

- **The zoom as a tool.** Generalise `river_zoom` to any hand. Integrate a
  heading θ(u) with a variable paper speed, at constant width and on a free
  course (his favourite was the push strip). Run it across the library, keep
  what yields, and say which hands don't.
- **Gradual scaling as composition.** A line or group whose scale changes
  across the sheet: the zoom used as a compositional move, not only along
  one line.
- **Blending and juxtaposition.** Hands set side by side and passing into one
  another, kept whole and never averaged (the round-8 rule).
- **Applying style.** One hand's grammar applied to another line's path (the
  river's course drawn in Hokusai's bends; a dance trail in the river's).
- **Aware self-overlaps.** Ian's note on the zoom piece: where the line comes
  back over itself, it replays or intermingles, or one side is subtracted.

Images: the zoom across hands (a specimen sheet), the strongest
scaling/blending piece, the style transfers, an aware-overlap close-up.

### C. Archaeology: past keeps brought into compositions

All four families, experimental, on a limb. Each one enters a composition in
a role it never had:
- Hand Drift / danced chain as the traverse (T2's crossing line);
- swell and bold gestures as the heavy intruder (T3), in place of bars;
- limb signs as figure flicker (T7), and the chorus as a multi-pen counterpart
  to ccolour;
- handover / accrue 3966 / belt as translate moves inside thread A's
  vocabulary.

The agent picks the order and may drop one that fights back. Images: one
piece per family that holds up, plus whatever surprised it.

### D. Measuring devices as a separate layer

A **catalogue**: each device is a function of (sheet, score, recipe metadata)
→ a layer, placed beside, under, over or in the margin of any drawing. Two
families, as Ian asked:
- **process-correlated** (his favourites "had direct correlations to your
  process, corpuses used"). New versions of the κ(s) chart, numbered atlas,
  margin chart, merged shadow, river taxonomy, specimen plate and ledger,
  reading what actually happened: which hands and corpora, the zoom
  schedule, which relations fired (thread A's score), the provenance of each
  line;
- **general**: rulers and scale bars, a density isoline, a heading rose,
  timing ticks along a line, survey or registration marks, a grid that misfits
  on purpose. Only the ones that are interesting.

Each device is one file, so they can be built in parallel. Images: the
catalogue sheet (each device on the same drawing), 2–3 paired pieces in the
round-10 sense, and the devices laid as a layer on round-14 sheets (4507,
14007).

## Risks

- **Too many agents in one checkout.** Each owns its own prefix; the
  contracts in 0c land before phase 1. Codex writers get explicit file lists.
- **The critic becomes the judge.** It flags; Ian decides. If it doesn't
  track round 14, it's dropped.
- **Archaeology as nostalgia.** A revived family must take a new role, or it's
  a museum. The test is the same as A's.
- **Devices as decoration.** A device must read something true about the
  drawing (its process, or its geometry), never ornament.
- **Overdoing 4507.** The mask is one move among many; the population must
  not become masks.
