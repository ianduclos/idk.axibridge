# Geometric measuring devices — 2026-10-19

Three general devices now read the finished drawing ink. Earlier device layers
are excluded. All output is unfilled pen polylines, including the lettering.
Pens are bounded to 0.15–0.30 mm. The preview is ready for Ian to check.

1. `arc_ticks` reads cumulative plotted arc length, starting again for each
   selected polyline. `which` chooses `longest` or `all_over`; `min_length`
   filters the latter. `every` sets the mm interval. `major_every` numbers every
   Nth tick and doubles its length. `tick`, `offset`, `label_h` and `pen` control
   the physical marks. It sits best over a drawing with room beside its lines.
   Ticks remain perpendicular. They choose a clear side, or a nearby gap within
   two minor tick lengths of the requested minimum offset. A crowded site with
   no such gap is omitted rather than drawn on ink. This specimen retains all
   **92 of 92** scheduled ticks along **1479.2868 mm** of ink. The numerical
   crossing check found none. Numbers report mm, not tick indices.

2. `survey_marks` locates the four drawing-box corners and the ink centroid.
   Coordinates are mm from the sheet origin. The centroid integrates segment
   length exactly; extra vertices do not bias it. The optional principal axis
   uses exact continuous second moments and separate short dashes. Isotropic
   ink gets no invented axis. Params are `cross`, `label_h`, `principal_axis`,
   `dash`, `gap` and `pen`. It sits best over the drawing, with space for labels
   around its bounds.

3. `misfit_grid` reads the gradient of Gaussian blurred line density. Its
   quadrature samples carry actual segment length. `pitch` is bounded to
   6–60 mm. `blur` sets Gaussian sigma. Positive `strength` attracts; negative
   strength repels. Displacement saturates and stays below 45% of pitch.
   `clear=0` keeps each grid line whole. Positive `clear` clips against exact
   ink-segment capsules, leaving contiguous polylines. `min_stub` drops short
   fragments. `pen` sets line width. It sits best underneath. A separate region
   gets a fitted view of the drawing; survey labels and arc readings retain
   their original sheet measurements.

The experiment uses the requested seed 4243 drift drawing, with hokusai and
meander69, length 6.0. A repelling grid sits underneath at 14 mm pitch, sigma
8 mm, strength −5 mm and 1 mm clearance. Ticks sit over it every 16 mm, with
numbers every 80 mm. Survey marks and the dashed principal axis also sit over.
The JSON beside the image contains the normalized recipe and all device specs.
The preview disables raster captions; every visible label is stroke text.

The grid's local recoil makes the empty spaces more specific. It is the most
useful addition in this image. The raw line remains easier to follow without
all the instruments. Around the lower-right triangular knot, several numbers,
crossings and the axis compete. The generator reaches y=0.2 and y=179.8 mm;
the preview clips parts of the corner crosshairs at the paper edges. A neutral
Sol review compared the final image with a zero-displacement control and the
bare drawing. It also found useful relationships in the warped grid and
congestion at the knot. This is one screen specimen, not paper validation.
