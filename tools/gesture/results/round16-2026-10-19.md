## Lead's read (written before Ian's notes; also on the page)

1. **Awareness is real and small.** The relations are in his register at 3–4×
   (lines go under, over, through, end at each other, take on another line's
   wiggle). On listen's sheets they barely show: the eight pairs look alike, and
   D's "unheard" device measured why: listening changed 2.7% of the ink. Built
   on sheets made for it (a jagged found line, a few long lines), sympathy reads
   at sheet scale: the loops crinkle where they cross the river (lead16-1). The
   lesson: awareness needs a src with something to copy.
2. **Distinct hands: solved, at a price.** B found the cause (every hand
   forced to the river's density, one camera for all, a zoom deeper than most
   hands have detail). v2's hands are visibly different characters; d′ 0.40 →
   0.90. The far-to-near zoom drama is mostly gone. The relay is the pick.
3. **Archaeology as relations works.** C's pool at a crossing is arch's single
   weight, now placed by contact; the coil at a crossing is the strongest.
   Risk: the pools are lobed, not the round head he loved. Stiffen and tremble
   are barely visible.
4. **Documents: two finds.** The staff (the score as a musical staff, on
   listen 15102's own score) and unheard (the drawing replayed with no
   relations, every change ringed). Provenance is a handsome document. The
   census and the sympathy trace are tools, not art.
5. **The lead's likely error this round.** Last round it was caution. This
   round the risk is the opposite: calling the awareness population a success
   because the mechanism is right, when the eye can't find it. I'm showing the
   population as it is and the built-to-be-seen sheets beside it.

## Contract gaps the threads found (for the synthesis bench, S0′)

1. A wildcard with exceptions (`except` ids or a role filter): the bridge
   wrote 156 named pairs to skip listen's void. (A, D)
2. Relations can't see a layer's pen width: tag marks with the renderer's pen
   in mm, as with `colour`. (A)
3. No dict param type: crossing's weights became five floats. (A)
4. A layer made only by relations (no part to grow and throw away). (C)
5. Marks a relation adds take their layer's renderer, so a restatement can't
   have its own pen. (C)
6. A sheet-wide budget across `*` pairs (C used `rctx.ground`).
7. Event `mark`/`s` refer to the dst as it was when the relation ran; later
   cuts shift them. A stable mark id, or `s` on the uncut layer. (D)
8. `synth.replay(drop=…)` for counterfactual documents (unheard does it by
   hand). (D)
9. `apply_devices` can't stack two beside panels compactly. (D)
10. A shared "measure a line against the library" helper. (B)
