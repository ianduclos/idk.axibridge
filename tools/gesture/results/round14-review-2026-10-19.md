## Where the lead was wrong, and why

1. **The lead ranked the densest sheets first again** (14012, 50011, 1464).
   That overcorrected round 13's lesson: Ian's dislike of tidy proofs read as "density is
   a quality". This round he rejects chaos without direction ("jumbled mess",
   "too chaotic" four times). He also rejects the sparse extreme ("boring"). What he
   keeps sits in the middle: 4507, 14007, and the uniform dud (14 moves on
   every sheet). **The bar is direction and elegance, not density.** Chaos is
   allowed only when it is executed with direction.
2. **The lead missed 4507 entirely.** What it does is a *partial, ambiguous mask*: a
   void in the middle that some layers respect and others cross, so the eye
   can't tell which layer is in front. That is T4/T5 from the composition note
   working as perception, not as structure. The lead read structure; Ian read
   the eye being tricked.
3. **The κ(s) chart has become a cliché inside compositions.** The lead treated
   it as reliable found matter. Ian wants measurement devices *separate*, and
   in more versions. That matches rounds 10–11, where measurement was "the
   strongest line so far" (numbered atlas, margin chart, merged shadow,
   taxonomy, ledger).
4. **The lead's dud was kept, for the fourth round running** (cgram uniform). Uniform
   sampling gave mid-density, cohesive sheets; the per-sheet temperament that
   was meant to add variety mostly added extremes.
5. **River: the lead was half right** (cap the zoom; the hand ladder is invisible).
   But the lead preferred the steered, framed piece, while Ian preferred the
   free course on a long strip at constant width. Width by zoom band was an
   unrequested ornament.

## Pattern

- Ian rewards **cohesion with a trick in it**: elegant line work, a middle density, and
  one perceptual ambiguity (a mask, interlocking layers).
- He penalises both ends of the density dial.
- Colour helps, as structure, not as a legend.
- Found matter wears out by repetition (the chart now, the far river before
  it). Rotate it, or move it to its own layer.
- Overlaps between parts of one line should be *aware* (replay, intermingle,
  subtract), never just a crossing.
