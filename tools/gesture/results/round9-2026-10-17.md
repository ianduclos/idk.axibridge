## Lead's read

1. The kit did its job: three of four threads used each other's modules
   without coordination (machine, diagram and dance voices dropped straight into
   the polyphonic arrangers).
2. The strongest new material is where a technique makes a grammar's own logic
   visible: the κ(s) of a shikasta line drawn by Matisse; a drift handing the pen
   to a stair or Koch machine; Noll+Nake as a hand; prosody's sparse stresses.
3. To check with Ian: the shikasta nib rows on the interlinear pages read
   strongly as script. They are recombined grammar tokens, not letters, but
   legibility is his call (never output legible Arabic).
4. The restatement sweep at 20–40 copies reads close to parallel bundles; keep
   restatement under the sign threshold in recipes.

## Proposed kit-core changes (from the threads)

1. Let a part see other parts' modulated marks (asked by three threads).
2. Token channels on the seed `hand`/`drift` voices (then `drift_tokens` goes).
3. Part labels in `summary()`; an arranger log in `Sheet.meta`; `Part.variant`.
4. A shared stroke-font helper; an ink-role convention (line, restate, bound, coil);
   a crop window in `kit.preview`; a `strs` param type.
