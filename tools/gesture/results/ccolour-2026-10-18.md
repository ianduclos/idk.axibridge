# Round 14 b4 `ccolour`: colour as structure (2026-10-18)

**Outcome.** One procedure, a composer called `ccolour`, makes a whole sheet per seed. It also makes a
pen plan for that sheet. The pens carry what a line is doing: riding, carrying, answering, being stained,
intruding. Each register owns a pen and lends it briefly. Across 24 seeds the sheets differ in frame,
number of pens (1–5), palette, which registers get colour, density and acts. Two or three sheets read as
drawings, not maps. The weak point: the core act is always `found_play`, so many sheets still centre on
one knot of made lines.

## What it does

- **The composition** (`decide`) is re-made per seed:
  - the frame, chosen on purpose from five (landscape, strip, square, portrait, small landscape);
  - a density dial with extremes (1 to 12 made lines);
  - an off-centre core, with the blank on the far side;
  - the found matter: the kappa chart, the Amazon stem at a per-sheet zoom (span 350–3600 channel
    widths, never the one fixed far view), or a machine line;
  - the made lines' hands and boxes: tight knots, middle boxes, long reaches into the blank;
  - optional acts: a container, a traverse (belt with a gain ramp, gmorph, passage, accrue's free hand
    seed 3966, handover), a counterweight (mutual soup, accrue knots, oxbows, a dense hand), and a heavy
    intruder (bar, L, stair, knot).
- **The core** is Study A's `found_play` (rules, catches, answers, cover), run with this sheet's
  matter and dials.
- **The pen plan** (`pen_plan`) is also re-made per seed:
  - how many pens, and which ones (Flexoki 600 and 400 hexes from the kit);
  - who owns what, in one of five modes:
    - `registers`: each register its own pen;
    - `lines`: made lines take turns over 2–3 pens;
    - `mono`: one pen; the found matter keeps a latent pen that shows only when it is carried away;
    - `inverted`: the found matter in the dark pen, the made lines light;
    - `pair`: two camps.
  - which event kinds may cross a colour over (carry, ride, answer, reverse, contact, spill);
  - an event budget per sheet and per line;
  - the minimum run of a borrowed pen (10–24 mm);
  - whether colour lies, that is, outlasts the carrying (`p_lie`, `lie_mm`, sometimes to the line's end).
- **A pen change is one line drawn by two pens.** The runs share their joint point, and every borrowed
  run lasts at least `min_run`. `_settle` swallows blips, so the colour never turns into confetti.
- **Every decision and event is logged** in `marks[0].meta["ccolour"]`, for example: "line 4 carries
  the chart … to the end".

## The three principles, and where each lands

- **T6, "Somewhere each borrows the other's behaviour … The borrowing is local and brief, not a
  style."** Registers are pens. When a made line catches the river or the chart, or plays the found
  contour it left (R2), that stretch is drawn in the lender's pen. A caught hand or an answer takes the
  other line's pen. The ride runs the other way: the found contour takes the line's pen back (`reverse`).
  The budget keeps it local: 2–7 events per sheet, at most 1–3 per line. In 4520 two black lines take
  the chart's blue where they carry it, and keep it to their ends: colour that lies.
- **T4, a frame inside the frame "that the drawing then refuses to obey completely."** The container
  stains what passes inside it with its own pen. A line that leaves keeps the stain for a while (the
  spill), and one line always refuses. Only lines that cross the edge are stained. In 4511 the purple
  container stains four red lines and the traverse, and the stain runs out into the margin.
- **T3, the heavy intruder.** One to three heavy marks (1.2–2.0 mm) sit at the core's edge and cut
  across it. They are straight, stepped or knotted where everything else curves. Weight and colour go
  together: the intruder takes the only foreign pen (`mono`, `pair`), or the darkest pen against light
  lines (`lines`, `inverted`). Examples: 4520's yellow stair and L, 4519's blue bars.

## Honest read against the bar

- **Different every time: partly.** Frame, palette, pen count, mode, density and acts all change, and
  the population does not look like one palette with one meaning. It does not read as a legend.
  But about half the sheets share one figure: a central tangle of `found_play` lines over a chart. The
  composer varies the distribution, not the vocabulary of the core act. That matches the lead's
  "series" note, but it leans toward a house style.
- **More dynamic: moderately.** The traverse, the heavy intruder and the weight contrast (0.2 mm hair
  against 0.7–1.4 mm bold) do the most work. The scale ladder is weaker than Oehlen's: there is no tiny
  periodic matter. The long traverse is often only one line.
- **Colour as structure: yes where the events are few and long.** It works in 4520 (lines keep the
  chart's colour), in 4507, and in mono sheets where the found matter's latent pen shows only in
  carried stretches.
- **Colour fails in three ways:**
  - light 400-level pens at 0.2 mm nearly vanish (true of pens, but weak on screen);
  - yellow is faint;
  - the `reverse` borrow rarely shows, because ridden stretches are short.
- **Density is kept.** Nothing is thinned for legibility.
- **Would Ian keep it?** Maybe 4520 and 4507 as drawings, and the pen plan as a method: colour that
  lies, a stain that spills, the intruder's foreign pen. The procedure is not yet a study. It needs a
  second core act (mutual soup or passage as the core instead of `found_play`) before the population
  stops resembling itself.
- **Flag.** 4516's counterweight, a dense hand in a small box, reads a little like cursive. It is not
  legible, but it needs a human read before any keep.

## Proposed core changes (not made)

- `found_play` could write the lender's id per point (which line's hand was caught). `colour`
  reconstructs it from the order of infections, which is approximate.
- `kit.contact` assumes equal image widths. Sheets with mixed frames need the tiler in `ccolour.py`; a
  shared version would help every composition thread.
- A shared pen-like (multiply) preview beside `oehlen_found.render_colour`, which paints colours
  opaquely in order.
