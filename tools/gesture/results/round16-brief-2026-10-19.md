## The project in three sentences

A research bank of drawn line "gestures" for a pen plotter (AxiDraw, black or
coloured pens on paper, judged here from rendered images only). Corpora of real
lines (artists, rivers, insect trails, dance, flies, seismograms) are measured
and turned into reusable techniques in a **kit**: voices draw centrelines,
modulators write per-point channels, renderers ink, arrangers place parts on a
sheet; a JSON recipe combines them deterministically. Ian (the artist) reviews
images and writes notes; what he keeps becomes a study in the app.

**What's new this round:** the next app bench (the synthesis bench) is built on
a **score**: a list of layers and the relations between them, played by an
executor. You write against it. Your awareness relations, revived techniques
and documents become the bench's parts, not another composer's private code.

## Shared contracts (lead-owned; import them, never edit them)

- **The score, version 2, and its executor**: `kit/_synth.py`. Read its
  docstring. `synth.run(score)` plays a score into an ordinary `Sheet`;
  `synth.played(sheet)` is the played score (layers, aggregated relations, the
  full event `log`, the `written` score); `synth.preview(sheet, path)` writes the
  colour image, the written score (`.recipe.json`, which `run` accepts) and the
  played score (`.played.json`). Layers are `part` (a kit part), `lines`
  (literal polylines; role `"unseen"` = heard but never inked) or `composer`
  (a whole v1 recipe sealed in a region).
- **Relations**: `kit/_relations.py`. Register with
  `@relation(id, phase="grow"|"contact", params=[P(...)])` in a file
  `relations_<prefix>*.py`. A relation gets `(rctx, src, dst, **params)` and
  returns `RelOut(dst=…, src=…, events=[…])`; the executor logs the events.
  Draw randomness only from `rctx.rng(*tags)`. Helpers: `crossings`, `cut`,
  `gap_marks`, `pick`. Reference relations in `relations_base.py`: `cross`
  (the control) and `yield` (round 15's).
- **The v1 score** (`_score.py`) and **devices** (`_devices.py`) are
  unchanged; devices read the played score from `notes["score"]` as before.
- Tests of the contract: `tools/gesture/tests/test_kit_synth.py`.

If the contract can't express what you need, **stop and say so in your
report** (what, and why); don't work around it inside your own files with a
private executor. The lead fixes the contract.

## Rules that bind (verbatim from docs/GESTURE-BANK-PROCESS.md §5)

- **Images only.** Research is judged from images; don't stage plots or touch
  the live app unless Ian asks.
- **Arabic calligraphy:** Qur'anic and devotional text is measure-only, never a
  generation source (the library's policy gate and `calligraphy.require_generative`
  enforce it); never output legible Arabic. A sheet that opens with a
  devotional invocation is measure-only.
- **Abstract script is never legible in any script**, Latin included.
- **Licences** are recorded, not used as a filter; every download needs Ian's
  go. No CAPTCHAs.
- **Taste:** contiguous lines, never hatching; distinct grammars kept whole,
  never averaged; transitions and less obvious sign-like shapes matter most;
  surprise from combination; systematic structure only as an accessory or an
  occasional event; masses as round beads and knots, not elongated capsules;
  digest sources conceptually, never copy their forms.
- **Determinism:** seeds derive from the score/recipe seed and part index
  only; never `hash()` or set order.
- **Processes:** worker pools only as context managers; check that none of
  yours survive.

Also:
- **No downloads** of any kind and no network use.
- **No stray marks** at the edge of a drawing; no capsules.
- **Legibility:** check by eye that no line reads as a letter or a word
  (round 15 flagged a "K", a "7", S/Z strokes).
