## Lead's read (written before Ian's notes)

Ranked by the relation between regimes, as the round-12 lesson asks.

1. **Contagion answers Ian's sentence most literally.** The line touches the
   river and climbs into the plate *as* a river. It touches the plate and drops
   down as a ruled, ticked axis, crosses the river again, sheds both and does
   its own loops. Ride versus catch is a real distinction. Weak points: the
   carried river is too even, and the ticked axis reads as a ruler. I see it as
   the core move for an Axis I study.
2. **Stages is where the two axes meet.** On sheets 1–3 the line catches the
   river's bends by riding it and carries them on to the next sheets. The
   counterfactual with the river moved away (row a) is a clean drawing on its
   own: hairline → nib loops. Sheet 4's pooled beads read as blots.
3. **Accrue has the round's only tipping point.** Its knot-to-rope passage is
   the best single moment on Axis II, a change from inside rather than from an
   encounter. The knot is a lumpy blot, and the ribbon after it is familiar.
4. **Gmorph is the cleanest morph of type within one hand.** Its dials could be
   a study's controls as they are. Risks: the sawtooth teeth are staggered
   barbs (an old dislike), and the hairpin curls flirt with letters.
5. **Handover proves the method but not the piece.** Its dud is the best
   evidence yet for "never average". The piece reads as four lines of
   handwriting. The specimen plate, with "boxes" and the coiled key, is the
   image to see.
6. **Rules is research more than drawing.** Its process strip makes "unnamed,
   breakable rules" visible. The piece is a cousin of found ground. Its best
   moment is a line that leaves the found and ends as a small river standing
   alone in the blank. Across hands the rules become a style.
7. **Mutual shows awareness running both ways, but only in its turn strip.**
   The sheet reads as a head in profile, haloed by the river. Its lasting
   lesson is the rate limits (refractory time, patience, one answer at a time):
   that is how mutual awareness stays legible.

**Pattern.** In four threads (rules, mutual, handover, accrue) the process
image is stronger than the piece. That fits Ian's love of the drawing beside its
own measurement, but it may also mean the pieces are not finished drawings yet.
Phase 2 has to make the relation readable without the strip.

**Possible combinations for phase 2.** Ian decides at the gate; these are not
decided.

- **Study A (Axis I).** The contagion catch, mutual's rate limits and refusal,
  and rules' "weight follows ground".
- **Study B (Axis II).** gmorph's dials or accrue's habit as the engine, with
  stages' "change only where it meets something" deciding *when*.
