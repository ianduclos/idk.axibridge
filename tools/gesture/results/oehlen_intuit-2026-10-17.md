# oehlen_intuit — an intuition-steered session after Oehlen's works on paper (round 12)

Thread: Ian-seeded, "some radical alternative approach, session self steered by intuition".
Working dir: `.local/gesture/out/oehlen_intuit/`. Code: `tools/gesture/experiments/oehlen_intuit*.py`.

## Session log

### 0 · Looking (before the digest)

Looked at all 29 sheets, the big 2016 charcoals (#20–24) longest.

- What I saw: very few lines on very large paper; a line that is slow and
  loose for half a metre and then does something *technical* without warning
  — two small square stair-steps (#24 bottom left), a rectangular notch like
  a cabinet profile (#22 top), a tight ∩∩∩ meander the size of a thumb in the
  middle of a two-metre sweep (#21). Lines doubled for a stretch and then
  parting. Lines that stop in air. Everything pushed to one side; the empty
  part is not background, it is half the drawing.
- In the dense ink sheets (#25, #13): a telephone-cord coil drawn as a line,
  little boxes in wobbly perspective, scribble balls, a road with lane dashes
  (#26), an illegible written word lying sideways.
- In the computer sheets (#17–19): the pixel staircase of a cheap mouse line,
  traced over by a hand that is not interested in it.
- What I felt: the thing that hits is not any shape but the **change of
  competence inside one line** — a hand that cannot draw a curve well
  suddenly drawing a ruler-straight step, then forgetting it again. And the
  feeling that nobody was steering towards a picture.
- My own bad hand: my competence is smooth parametric curves and the kit.
  The clumsy equivalent of Oehlen's mouse is **typing coordinates blind** —
  writing a polyline as numbers from the gut, no function, no preview. That
  is where I start.

### 1 · s01 — typed blind
Three polylines typed as numbers from the gut, 0–100 units, no function, no
preview (`TYPED_A/B/C`). **Saw:** a floor plan, a coastline drawn by a child;
a crenel notch, a row of tiny teeth. Typing gives orthogonal moves by itself
— my bad hand is blocky, a cheap-mouse hand. **Felt:** the tiny lost teeth
are the best thing; everything is one speed, slow. Nothing fast in it.

### 2 · s02 — looking for something uninterested
Wanted a fast line that is neither mine nor a "hand". The stork GPS (Sicily,
Oduella), 12 windows of 500 fixes, rng 4113. **Saw:** a nest knot of GPS
jitter with dead-straight flight rays; one slow migration arc with a knot at
its head. **Felt:** the stork already changes competence inside one line —
jitter knot / ruler flight. Took it without deciding why.

### 3 · s03 — stork arc slammed across the typed drawing (1.4 mm)
**Saw:** a map with a road. Two lines = a road. **Felt:** too legible,
tasteful. The knot at its head reads as an insect. Dud, kept going.

### 4 · s04 — knots only
Twelve 300-fix windows where the stork stayed within 30–400 m (rng 4114).
**Saw:** starbursts. GPS error at the nest. **Felt:** bored by the stork as
a found object; it looks like a found object.

### 5 · s05 — typed again, trying to type a loose charcoal line
`TYPED_L1..L3`, 300 × 200. Smoothed L1 with Chaikin. **Saw:** smoothing
turned my clumsy numbers into a clip-art swoosh — a logo. The raw L2 (thin,
hesitant) and the bottom crawl with two square teeth (a horizon with a tiny
factory) are honest. **Felt:** smoothing is my competence. The swoosh is
the wrong thing. Decided to *leave* it and fight it rather than fix it.

### 6 · s06 — cut the swoosh where it crosses itself, burn a stork knot there
Too clever and too small (knot 10 mm). Nothing happened. **Dud: timid.**

### 7 · s07 — not timid: the knot big (inner 80 % = 22 mm radius), in a dumb place, sitting on the horizon
**Saw:** a thorn-bush of straight segments; one flight ray, thin and
dead straight, comes in from the left edge and slices the swoosh — the best
line on the page and nobody drew it. **Felt:** first real shock. The nest is
drawn by the bird's error. The swoosh now looks embarrassed, which is right.

### 8 · s08 — a feeler typed while *looking* at s07
`TYPED_L4`, a hesitant thin line from top-left to the knot. Clipped all to
the frame. **Felt:** it is turning into a polite composition — every element
in its own region. I am composing.

### 9 · s09 — turn the sheet
Rotated 90° clockwise, 200 × 300 portrait. **Saw:** the swoosh stopped being
a swoosh — it falls now; the horizon became a tall stiff wall with a small
door-notch, the knot pressed against it. **Felt:** "oh". This is the turn of
the session. The drawing became someone else's drawing, sideways.
(Practical side: it is drawn on the machine in landscape, 300 × 200 inside the
AxiDraw's 300 × 218, and seen in portrait. The plotter draws it the wrong way up.)

### 10–11 · s10, s11 — far river, near river
A Greenberg–Ganti river centreline (Uda, 64 km, 30 m pixel steps) shrunk to
a thumb (Ian's ruling: far away, tight zigzag), and a 2 km stretch enlarged
hoping the 30 m pixels would show as a computer staircase. **Saw:** a
caterpillar on the swoosh's tail, and a nervous squiggle; no staircase.
**Felt:** decoration. Removed both. **Dud.**

### 12 · s12 — subtract the swoosh, the one nice thing
**Saw:** a thistle. One weight, legible, a thing. **Felt:** the fight left
with the swoosh. The wrong thing was the opponent the rest needed. It has
to stay. (This is the learning of the session, I think.)

### 13 · s13 — swoosh back in the fine pen; a fat pen (1.6 mm) over only its first third
**Saw:** the fat stretch at the top is a charcoal side-stroke, heavy, then
the line thins and forgets itself. *Accident:* shapely split the swoosh at
its self-crossing when clipping, so a second fat stretch landed on the cute
"e"-loop and made it more legible. **Felt:** dislike it. Let it stay a while
— that is the brief.

### 14 · s14 — something stupid: a typed arrow pointing at nothing
**Saw:** the arrow laughs. It turns the sheet into a diagram that has lost
its subject; it points at the gap between the swoosh and its loop.
**Felt:** more than expected — but it is a gag, the most obvious sign there
is, and Ian has asked for less obvious signs. Holding it.

### 15 · s15 — the staircase I failed to get from the river
The fat stretch redrawn as a cheap-mouse pixel line (3 mm cells): smooth thin
line → fat staircase → thin again, inside one line. **Saw:** the change of
competence I saw in the charcoals on the first look, at last, and on my own
line. The accident from step 13 became a stepped bracket on the soft loop.
**Felt:** strong — and uneasy: the pixel line is close to Oehlen's own
computer-line *form*. It got there through my process (the computer degrading
its own competent curve), but Ian should judge whether it reads as a quote.
The arrow (s15 vs s15b): without it the sheet is more serious and more
elegant; with it, stupider. Undecided.

### 16 · s16 — sheet 2? typed as fast as I could, a scribble
**Saw:** a polygonal spiral, and then — again — the square notch. When I
"scribble" I make a spiral; I can't make chaos, the stork can. The notch is
my tic (it is in A, in L3, here).

### 17 · s17 — the spiral big, fat and pixelated, a stork flight through it
**Saw:** a maze, a key, a video game. **Dud.** Pixels as a *style* are a
cliché; as an *event* inside one line (s15) they work. The thin stork
flights (a fan, a long triangle) were lovely and wasted here.

### 18 · s18 — the edge
The knot's rays were all cut on one vertical at the left edge = a drawn
edge (Ian: no stray marks polluting the edge). Tried breaking lines at their
last inside vertex instead, and dropping the feeler and stub. **Saw:** the
best line — the flight ray — vanished: its first vertex was outside the
frame. Cleaner, poorer.

### 19 · s19 — ragged cut ends (rng 4120+i): cut, then pull each cut end back 3–25 mm
**Saw:** the ray is back. *And an accident:* shapely, clipping the knot,
split it at every one of its own crossings and threw away every piece
shorter than 0.5 mm. The knot has a white heart now: a hole punched in the
drawing, flakes around it, like a cover pasted over the middle and torn off.
**Felt:** the best thing that happened all session, and I did not do it.
Let it stay. But it was 314 confetti fragments.

### 20 · s20 — making the accident on purpose: an irregular heart, pen lifted inside
**Saw:** a bullet hole; a drawn circle. **Felt:** competent again — when I
make the accident deliberately it turns into the effect. Discarded.

### 21 · s21 — the accident's own logic, kept contiguous
Split the knot at its own crossings, lift every piece shorter than τ, keep
the rest joined (τ 2 → 204 runs; τ 3 → 122 runs, median 5.6 mm). **Saw:**
τ 3 is a clean burst; τ 2 keeps flakes inside the hole, like charcoal debris.
**Felt:** τ 2. The hole is where the bird crossed itself most — that is a
reason, not a parameter.

### 22–23 · s22, s23 — arrow out; the weakest line back in a wrong colour
Arrow dropped: a gag, and the most obvious sign there is. The sheet was now
elegant, which worried me. Brought back the weakest line I had removed (the
typed feeler) in a second pen, blue-400. **Saw:** blue + wandering line =
a river on a map. Wrong kind of wrong (and against Ian's river ruling in
spirit). Red-400: a fuse running into the burst. **Felt:** a stupid little
story, but the colour stops the sheet being tasteful. Kept red.

### 24 · s24 — stopping short
The tail after the pixel hook was a flourish — finishing. The last swoosh
piece keeps only 62 % of its length and stops in the air.

### 25 · sheet 2, three rounds of typed lines only (110 × 210, a door-shaped strip)
Round 1: a crack. Round 2 (typed looking at it): a line that wants to cross
the crack and turns back just before — a beak, a mouth eating the crack.
Round 3: one fast line in long strides that loses its nerve in a tiny zigzag
— and the whole thing became a stick figure. **Dud, and the second learning:**
my bad hand alone makes circuits and stick figures. Sheet 1 worked because
something indifferent (a stork's GPS error, a clipping library's noding) did
the parts I could not want. Stopped sheet 2 there.

## The finished piece

**`oehlen_intuit-1-sheet1.jpg`** — 200 × 300 mm seen portrait; drawn on the
AxiDraw in landscape, 300 × 200 inside its 300 × 218 (recipe `turn: false`),
so the machine draws it on its side. Frame chosen because the turn (step 9)
is what made the drawing; the proportion is the charcoals' 2 : 3. Three pens:
0.3 mm fine liner (swoosh, knot), 0.4 (the typed wall), 1.6 mm marker (the two
pixel stretches), 0.5 red-400 (the feeler). 209 inks, 201 of them the knot's
runs (the hole makes pen lifts; each run is one contiguous line).

Kit: `voice oehlen_intuit_sheet` + `renderer oehlen_intuit_pens`
(`kit/voices_oehlen_intuit.py`), sources frozen in
`library/typed_drawing/oehlen_intuit_sheet1` (typed numbers + 300 stork fixes
in local metres, CC0). Recipe beside the image, seed 4127. The kit replay
draws its own ragged cut ends (`ctx.rng`), so it differs from session step
s24 in where a few cut lines stop; nothing else.

## Honest read

Two things I would defend: the session found its own reasons (the swoosh
had to stay as the opponent; the hole is where the bird crossed itself most;
the sheet is the drawing turned), and the sheet is not in the project's
vocabulary — no hands, no beads, no drift, no plate. The log is not a story
told afterwards: the duds (s03, s06, s11, s17, s20, sheet 2) were felt as
duds at the time and are kept in the record.

Would Ian keep it? I think the sheet has a real chance — it has the
uncomfortable mix he asked for in "an experimental approach" and it is a
bold, sparse sheet with one mass. The risks, plainly: (a) the pixel
staircase sits close to Oehlen's own computer-line form; (b) the knot's
201 runs, ~3–5 mm at the rim, may read as the confetti he dislikes; (c) the red
line reads as a fuse into an explosion — figurative, a joke; (d) the burst
itself is close to a cartoon "impact". If he drops one, I would drop (c)
first (`colour: black` or `feeler: false` in the recipe) and keep the rest.
The process is the more transferable result than the sheet: blind-typed lines
as an incompetent hand, plus an indifferent found process, plus keeping the
accidents of tools (clipping, noding) instead of reproducing them on purpose.
