## Read against the lead's

1. **Agreed: the studies are correct but not dynamic.** The lead saw this only
   for B ("quieter than gmorph's hands sheet"). Ian sees it for both. A's
   density did not make it dynamic: six lines catching and answering still
   share one look from sheet to sheet. Its dynamics are local events, not
   composition.
2. **The unit is wrong.** Both studies are a mechanism with a seed. Ian asks
   for *full compositions that are different every time*. That means the
   composition itself (where the found ground sits, how many regimes, which
   techniques, the scale, the frame, the blank) has to vary and decide,
   instead of a fixed layout with a varying line. The same failure as round
   10's "collapsing into a style", one level up: a fixed composition
   collapses into a template.
3. **Colour reads.** The diagnostic map, meant as process evidence, is the
   image he commented on as a picture. The colour coding of what a line is
   doing has some value as a drawing in its own right. This fits his history:
   he liked the drawing beside its measurement.
4. **The river is wearing out.** He loves the Amazon river, but it is
   "starting to be overused". The far-view GRWL stem has been in found ground,
   rules, contagion, mutual, stages and Study A. The next round must not lean
   on it.
5. **A new Axis II idea from him: zoom along the line.** Small dense twists
   that gradually get larger, then smaller. This is a change of scale along
   one line, not of hand. gmorph's `gain` and the belt's tightness touched it,
   but neither changed the scale of the whole gesture vocabulary
   continuously.
