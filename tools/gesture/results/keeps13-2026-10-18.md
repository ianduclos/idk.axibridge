Choices I made:

- **Revisit is two studies, not one with a dance control.** The piece and the
  variant differ in frame (portrait vs landscape), and a frame is not a
  control. The variant records `limb_signs` as its parent.
- **The old-beside-new layout is not offered.** The round-7 tree is old
  experiment code (`signs_study.events_sheets`) with a fitted grammar, not a
  kit recipe, so offering it would mean freezing large old code. The notes say so.
- **"Which found plates" and "the river on/off" are one control**, plus a
  plate-kind control. `found_on` (all / first / second) pastes the plate and
  the river, the plate only, or the river only. Each part keeps its index,
  seed and pasted box. The plate control picks the measure_next plate.
- **Cover count/kind is one control, `cover_use`.** The piece's covers are
  composed, not searched: a torn cut strip and a plastic tint sheet. So the
  control filters them (both / cut / tint / none) or puts both in one mode
  (all-cut / all-tint). Every polygon is still drawn first, so the cover rng
  runs as composed.
- **Tightness at the end is my addition to the belt.** It is the `belt`
  voice's `gain_end`, which ramps the gain along the line. Ian asked to
  explore "line that morphs gesture type". The default (end = start = 1.8)
  draws the kept row exactly.

## Multi-pen: voices by ink (app change)

Both pieces change pen by region or by mark: blue found layer, green-400
tint, black gesture; black and red limbs. Before this change a study could
keep pens only per part (the chorus). Now a study may list `inks`, with
`keep: "voices"`. Its voices are then the Flexoki pens the kit inks name.

- Keep makes a group with one layer per pen. An ink the drawing does not use
  this time is not kept: `found_ground` keeps 3 layers, because no made line
  gets tinted green.
- Resolve is still the single path. Each voice layer resolves the same pinned
  recipe and keeps its own ink.
- The pen widths of the research (blue 0.3 mm, black 0.9 mm) are not carried
  over. Pens are matched by colour, as for the chorus.
