## The project in three sentences

A research bank of drawn line "gestures" for a pen plotter (AxiDraw, black or
coloured pens on paper, judged here from rendered images only). Corpora of real
lines (artists, rivers, insect trails, dance, flies, seismograms) are measured
and turned into reusable techniques in a **kit**: voices draw centrelines,
modulators write per-point channels, renderers ink, arrangers place parts on a
sheet; a JSON recipe combines them deterministically. Ian (the artist) reviews
images and writes notes; what he keeps becomes a study in the app.

## How you will be judged (read this twice)

- **Dynamics, not legibility.** Contrast of scale, speed, density and weight
  inside one sheet and between sheets. Density is a quality: the lead ranked
  tidy single-relation proofs first in round 13 and Ian liked the dense "duds"
  (soup, runaway, crowded) instead. Do not thin a sheet out to make it readable.
- **The relation between regimes**, never measurable novelty. A sound statistic
  is not a taste signal.
- **A fixed layout with a varying line is a template** (round 13 phase 2). Where
  your thread makes compositions, the composition itself must decide and vary.
- Numbers are welcome as evidence of what happened; they are not the argument.

## Rules that bind (verbatim from docs/GESTURE-BANK-PROCESS.md §5)

- **Images only.** Research is judged from images; don't stage plots or touch
  the live app unless Ian asks.
- **Arabic calligraphy:** Qur'anic and devotional text is measure-only, never a
  generation source (the library's policy gate and `calligraphy.require_generative`
  enforce it); never output legible Arabic. A sheet that opens with a
  devotional invocation is measure-only.
- **Abstract script is never legible in any script**, Latin included; a page
  with an Arabic look gets a human reader's check before it is kept.
- **Licences** are recorded, not used as a filter (private research); every
  download still needs Ian's go (name, source, size). No CAPTCHAs.
- **Taste** (from Ian's notes; details in the review files):
  - contiguous lines, never hatching;
  - distinct grammars kept whole, never averaged;
  - transitions and less obvious sign-like shapes matter most;
  - surprise comes from combination, for example two voices on one surface,
    or a drawing beside its own measurement;
  - systematic structure only as an accessory or an occasional event;
  - masses as round beads and knots, not elongated capsules;
  - digest sources conceptually, never copy their forms.
- **Determinism:** seeds derive from the recipe seed and part index only; never
  `hash()` or set order (a `max(set(...))` tie once made sheets differ between
  processes).
- **Processes:** worker pools only as context managers; agents check that none
  of theirs survive (22 orphans once piled up from earlier runs).
- **Freeze from HEAD**, never from files another agent may be editing.

Also:
- **No downloads** of any kind (corpora, models, packages) and no network use.
  If you need something that is not on disk, say so in your report.
- **No stray marks** polluting the edge of a drawing; no capsules.
- **Oehlen images stay out of git.** Any image that contains an Oehlen
  thumbnail goes to `.local/gesture/out/round14-refs/<your prefix>/`, never into
  `tools/gesture/results/`. His procedures transfer; his looks don't (no
  Oehlen-looking trees, posters, spray, pixel staircases).
