# Round 16 thread A `aware`: awareness as composable relations (2026-10-19)

**Outcome.** Five awareness relations now run on the v2 score, with no private executor. They are
`crossing`, `sympathy`, and three new ones: `hesitate`, `accompany` and `make_room`. A bridge turns any
listen sheet into a v2 score: listen's history stays, and its weave/yield moves out into the score.
At sheet scale the awareness is nearly invisible. The population looks like round 15. At 3–4× it is
there: lines go under, over, through, end at each other, or bend. That is the register Ian asked
for, but it also means the effect only tells up close. The contract held. Two small gaps are listed at
the end.

### `crossing` (contact)

Every crossing gets its own outcome, drawn from weights in the order `crossings()` returns:
- `gap_under`: the dst breaks (it passes under).
- `gap_over`: the src breaks.
- `cross`: both run through.
- `stop`: one line ends at the other. It is the line whose tail beyond the crossing is shorter, and only
  when no other crossing lies on that tail. The tail must be at most `stop_max` (25 mm), and the
  line keeps at least `min_left` (12 mm). It ends exactly on the other line, as a T.
- `sympathy`: no gap. Within `reach` (5 mm) of the crossing, the dst is carried `lean` (0.8 mm) along
  the src's drawing direction and takes on the src's wiggle. It reads as a small swerve. It is
  allowed only when no other crossing on that dst lies within reach, so it never moves another gap.

`streak` (0.45) is the chance that the last outcome repeats. Gaps are sized in pen widths: each side
is `pen × (0.5 + gap)`, and `jitter` varies each gap by up to 30%.

Three guards came from looking at close-ups:
- `tangle` (4 mm): crossings of the same two marks this close are one knot. The knot gets one
  outcome, at its first crossing.
- `min_dash` (8 mm): no gap may leave a piece shorter than this.
- `min_piece` (2.5 mm): a cut never leaves a stray end.

Without these, a line crossing a crowd turned into a dashed line.

Over the population (8 sheets, 916 crossings):
- through 59% (this includes knots and fallbacks);
- a gap, under or over, 31%;
- bend 6%;
- stop 4%.

So most crossings are not gaps, as Ian asked.

### `sympathy` (contact)

This is lcorp's `aware_marks` sympathy, lifted to work over any two layers. The bending line takes on
the other's own wiggle, which is the other minus its 3 mm smoothing. It reads the sample straight
across, not the nearest one. The effect fades with a raised cosine over `reach` (4 mm). It is then
smoothed over 0.8 mm, so no kink appears. `gain` is 0.8, which is subtle. Who bends is a param:
`later` (the default), `dst`, `src` or `shorter`. A line continued in another pen (the bridge splits
a listen line by ink) is not heard near the join.

### Three of my own

- **`hesitate`.** A line grows unsure as it closes in on another. The strength is nearness times the
  rate of approach, so running calmly beside another line does nothing. The result is a fine tremor
  (0.16 mm, wavelength about 1.3 mm, with drifting phase). It starts 6 mm before the meeting and
  settles over 2.5 mm after.
- **`accompany`.** Where the dst already runs beside the src (within 6 mm, within 25°), it holds the
  distance it arrived at for about 22 mm, copying the src's course. Then it lets go. Each such run
  is taken up with chance 0.7.
- **`make_room`.** The past makes room for the future. When a line is placed, an earlier line that
  passes within 2 mm of it, without crossing, bows away. It is the only one of the five that rewrites
  the src. The bow fades out near crossings and near the line's own ends, since an end that flicks
  reads as a hook.

### The listen bridge

`BridgeComposer` is listen's `Composer` with `overlaps()` as a no-op. `compose()` runs it exactly as
`listen()` does: same ctx, same parts. Each listen `Layer` becomes one `lines` layer per ink, in
drawing order, with its own `{"id": "wire", "pen": L.pen}` renderer.

Relations are written `*`→`*`. A sheet with listen's void gets named pairs that skip the void
instead, as listen did; that is the first contract gap, below. listen's own relations (imitate, echo
and so on) ride along in `decisions.listen_relations`.

## Duds

- **`make_room`, first version.** It stacked pushes from several later layers into a 3 mm S-swerve
  and flicked a line end into a hook. It is now capped at 0.6 × room, tapered at the ends, and set
  to room 2 mm and strength 0.6. The swerve in row 3 of image 4 is still the loudest of the five.
- **`hesitate`, first version.** It trembled all along any line that ran beside another, which
  looked like a different line style. Weighting it by the rate of approach fixed that.
- **`crossing` before `tangle`/`min_dash`.** It cut lines into dashes wherever a line ran through a
  knot or a crowd. That is exactly the risk the brief named.

## My honest read

- **`sympathy` is in Ian's register.** It is felt, not seen, and it works between any layers. I think
  he would keep it. But on listen's smooth hands there is little wiggle to inherit. It shows only
  where a wiggly line (the river, a hand's hooks) is crossed or run along. It would sing on lcorp's
  or a corpus hand's texture.
- **`crossing` answers his note directly.** Mostly lines run through. Under and over come
  unpredictably, a few lines stop at each other, and a few bend. The streaks read as a pair of layers
  having a habit. At sheet scale it is quieter than round 15's weave/yield, because fewer gaps are
  cut. He may say "I can't see it". The hard sheet shows where the top of the range is.
- **Of my three, `make_room` is the strongest idea.** The earlier line seems to have known what was
  coming. It is also the one most likely to be named rather than felt.
- **`accompany` is rare on listen,** 1–7 per sheet. It needs layers that already run alongside.
- **`hesitate` is honest but small.** At sheet scale it is grain.
- **Would he keep it?** The relations, yes, as parts for the bench, `sympathy` and `crossing` first.
  The sheets themselves are listen's sheets. Awareness alone does not make a new drawing.
- **Legibility.** These are listen's own shapes, unchanged: 15106's red "7" top-left, 15102's black
  "Z"-step at right. My relations add no new letterforms.

## Contract notes (proposed core changes, not made)

1. **A wildcard with exceptions.** `src: "*"` cannot say "every layer but the void". The bridge works
   around it with named pairs, which is correct but verbose: 156 relations for 15102 (78 pairs × 2 kinds) instead of 2. A
   proposal: `"except": [ids]` on a relation, or a role filter (`"roles": [...]`).
2. **Relations cannot see a layer's pen width.** Gaps should scale with the over line's real pen
   (listen's bold voice is 0.6–1.0 mm, its listeners 0.35–0.45 mm). `crossing` takes a `pen` param
   instead. A proposal: the executor tags marks with the layer renderer's `pen` (mm), the way it
   already tags `colour`.
3. **No dict param type.** The design doc's `"weights": {...}` became five bounded floats
   (`w_gap_under`, …). That is fine, and it is listed here only because the doc shows a dict.

Everything else needed (src rewriting by contact relations, `rctx.ground` order for "later", event
fields, determinism by `rctx.rng`) was there and worked.
