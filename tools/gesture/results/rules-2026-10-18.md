# rules — rules from the sheets, played as breakable rules (round 13, thread I-a, 2026-10-18)

**Outcome.** I read nine candidate rules off the 29 Oehlen sheets. Each one is
stated as a relation between layers. Two are falsified by the sheets, one is
too weak to use, and five are implemented as breakable rules in a new
arranger, `rules`. The piece is one sheet, 300 × 200 mm, at seed 1317. A
dancer's thrusting hand and a river-bend hand are played by the five rules
over the project's own found matter: a κ(s) chart and the far river. Every
event goes into a log, each with a place and a reason. The process strip
makes that log readable. The risk is real: across hands, the rules start to
read as a style (image 3).

Images only. Nothing was plotted or downloaded, and I made no git writes. The
Oehlen thumbnails sheet is in `.local/gesture/out/round13-refs/rules/oehlen-29-numbered.jpg`,
not in git. The numbers below are its filename prefixes; there is no 10.

Honesty notes:
- **R2 is partly projection.** In 13 and 14, the motif in the blank echoes
  the found: the staff rows and the stepped paste edge. In 03 and 27 it does
  not resemble anything found. So the sheets support "turns periodic in the
  blank". "Becomes the last found it touched" is my extension. It is also the
  part that answers Ian's "the line in front becomes the river".
- **R3 belongs to the charcoals.** No collage sheet does it.
- **R1 belongs to the collage-drawings.** With no found layer, the charcoals
  cannot test it.
- **Two rules never meet on the same sheet in Oehlen.** R1 and R3 come from
  different bodies of work. Playing them together is my composition, not his.

## What I tried

- **r1** (1301): three hands over the whole sheet, with a pasted rectangle as
  "the found". The rectangle made nearly every line heavy. Every rule fired
  everywhere. This became the dud.
- **R2's first version** added the found contour's wiggle as a lateral
  offset. On the river it read as tremor, and on the straight chart rows it
  did nothing. I replaced it with **becoming**: the found contour's own
  turning, handed over along the line from where the line last touched it,
  starting at the nearest stretch that turns most. This produces a little
  river in the blank, or a chart sawtooth.
- **The exit test** used the rectangles at first. The river's rectangle is
  mostly empty paper, so "the blank" was misjudged. Exits are now measured
  from found ink (10 mm).
- **Sweeps:** 6 hand pairs × 3 seeds, then 16 seeds of the chosen pair, then
  8 seeds each of 4 more pairs (all in `.local/gesture/out/rules/`).
  `dance_thrusting` + `river_bend` with a decided blank (the right third) was
  the one that read as a drawing.
- **The cover's outline** is drawn in the found pen. Without it, the cut was
  as subtle as in round 12. With it, the strip reads as pasted paper.

## Honest read

**Axis I test: yes, in at least four places.**
- **Weight follows ground.** The thrusting line is heavy exactly while it
  crosses the chart rows, and light between and outside them.
- **R3 broke, and the line became the graph.** Lower right of the chart, the
  river-bend line is born out of a chart spike and traces it down before it
  goes its own way.
- **R4 broke.** Lower left, a heavy straight stretch rides the bottom chart
  row.
- **The line ignores the cover.** The cover strip cuts all four chart rows,
  and the dancer's line crosses it twice, uncut, as if it were not there.
- **The line goes its own way.** The river-bend line crosses the river,
  leaves, and loops wide through the blank lower right.

**Axis II test: one place.** The river-bend line's last 70 mm. It changes
from a smooth hand arc, through a loose wave, into a tight river meander, and
stops in the air. The change starts about 10 mm after it leaves the river's ink and
runs toward its end. The middle, the loose wave, is its own thing. But it is
short. A viewer could read it as a flourish.

**Would Ian keep it?**
- The process strip, probably. It answers his question directly: here are
  the unnamed rules, written down, and here is where each one fired or broke.
- The piece, maybe not as a finished thing. It sits close to *Found ground*:
  the same blue found matter and a black negotiating line. Its new moves are
  the weight that follows the ground, the line born from the graph, and the
  ending that becomes the river.
- The risk he named is visible in image 3. With five rules on, the sheets
  start to share a look: heavy over blue, a step at the edge, a little river
  at the end. **The rules are becoming a style.**
- Better next steps:
  - fewer rules per sheet;
  - a rule that fires once and is then retired (H6, which held in the
    charcoals);
  - Oehlen's own move from A7: try contradictory rules on purpose.

**Flaws.**
- The chart sawtooth that line 1 becomes (R2 broke) lands in the busy centre
  and adds clutter there.
- Line 2 carries a small hairpin from its own hand, mid-sheet near the
  spike. It is a thin hairline, not a mass, but it is capsule-shaped.

## Files

- `tools/gesture/gesture/kit/arrangers_rules.py`: the arranger `rules`. It
  reuses the `oehlen_found` helpers (Ground, join, covers, inks) and adds
  R1–R5, the log, and `become` / `feature_start`. The renderer is
  `oehlen_found_ink`.
- `tools/gesture/experiments/rules.py`:
  - recipes: `piece`, `rulebook`, `raw`, `dud_crowded`, `r1`, `r2`;
  - sweeps and `overlay` (event rings and rule colours);
  - `review` rebuilds all four images:
    `.local/gesture/.venv/bin/python tools/gesture/experiments/rules.py review`.
- `tools/gesture/tests/test_kit_rules.py`: 13 tests. They cover each rule
  firing and breaking, the no-break mode, R1's weight against found ink, R3's
  edge entry, R5 cutting, `become` / `feature_start`, and determinism across
  processes (PYTHONHASHSEED 1 / 77). `test_kit.py` still passes (54 together).

## Proposed core changes (not made)

- In `arrangers_oehlen_found.negotiate`, make listening a callback, not just
  windows. I had to copy its ride loop to make curvature decide when the line
  listens.
- `Ground` silently drops short lines. If it kept an owner index per line, a
  ride could say which found part it rode without rebuilding that list.
- `render_colour` / `FLEXOKI` have no grey. A neutral (base-500) would let
  process panels show "the hand before the rules" without borrowing a pen
  colour.
