# Integration after round 16: listen with distinct-hand voices, all awareness on (2026-10-19)

Ian, round 16: the relay "yess thats whats up. we need to integrate this type
of line with the aware compositions and the complex ones". Then: "build listen
with the distinct-hand (hdist) lines as its voices on the v2 score, all
awareness relations on, and show me a population."

**What it is.** `experiments/integ16.py`. listen's composer grows every
speaker, listener and refuser line through one method; here that method draws
the same hand through `hdist_zoom` (each hand at its own scales). The rest of
listen is unchanged: the same history (imitate, exaggerate, translate, refuse,
echo, calm, nest, bind, the void), with its own weave/yield removed. The
sheet is a v2 score with all five awareness relations (sympathy, crossing,
hesitate, accompany, make_room) played by the executor. Seeds 15101–15108,
consecutive, unpicked; 0.3–1.7 s per sheet.

## Lead's read (before Ian's notes)

- **The line character changed more than anything else.** listen's angular
  hooks are mostly gone; each sheet now carries several kinds of line at once
  (15105: a bold blue zigzag over fine crinkled trails; 15103: magenta loops
  against black knots). This is round 15's main risk answered: listen's sheets
  no longer share one texture.
- **The compositions stay listen's.** Frames, the speakers' placement, the
  doubled imitations (15106's red runs) and the void are all still there, so
  this reads as the same composer with new voices.
- **Strongest:** 15102 (open, a blue river-like line threading a black
  knot field), 15108 (one long magenta arc tying the sheet), 15104 (the bold
  black arc as the single weight over fine trails).
- **Risks:** 15103 is the densest sheet listen has made (looping hands fill
  more of the box): close to "jumbled". The spliced relations are harder to
  point at, because the hdist lines loop on their own. Awareness is again
  subtle at sheet scale: middle and right columns differ only up close
  (sympathy at 15106's red-on-black run, gaps at crossings).
- **Not done here:** the speakers are single hands, not relays; a relay
  speaker (one line changing hands) is the obvious next variant. hdist's
  `drawn_mm` per listen length (200 mm per R) was set once, not tuned.

## Balance, first try

3. `integ16-3-balance.jpg`: only the speakers are distinct hands; listeners,
   refusers and the later small layers keep listen's own lines. All awareness
   on. Same seeds.

Lead's read: it helps where listeners carry much of the sheet (15102, 15106,
15108: two kinds of line, the angular listeners against the looping
speakers). It barely helps where the speakers carry most of the ink (15103,
15105 stay busy). So "which layers" is a weak lever on its own; the stronger
one is probably how much of the sheet distinct hands may occupy: one
distinct-hand line per sheet, or a share dial (none → one → speakers → all),
which is also the form a balance control takes on the synthesis bench.
