# Round 16 C `dig`: archaeology further, revivals as v2 relations (2026-10-19)

**Outcome.** Five early techniques now exist as relations in the v2 score, so the synthesis bench can use them. Each takes a role it never had: it happens to one layer *because of* another. Four hold up: **pool**, **restate**, **tremble** and **stiffen**. **Knot** fought back and is the dud. The surprise is how well they sit together. One score with an echo, tremor on contact, one machine stair and one pool gives quiet, elegant sheets at a middle density. In each, a single heavy weight sits at a junction (`dig-1`). Everything is in `relations_dig.py` plus one renderer, `dig_wet`. No private executor was needed.

## What I revived, and in what new role

| # | relation | revived from | old role | new role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dig_pool` (contact) | rounds 6–7 regimes and wet edges; round 9 `coil` (never reviewed) | a mass grown by a line's own regime field or a sign operator; coil as a renderer for stressed stretches | **a mass placed by contact.** Where two layers cross, one of them pools: line → nib → round wet mass and back (round 7's measured edge profile), or (variant) a coil wound out from the crossing as one contiguous spiral. A `sheet_max` budget makes it *the single weight*. Round 6 measured that 84 % of the reference's masses sit on junctions. Here that finding is the rule. |
| 2 | `dig_restate` (grow) | round 9 `restate` (siyah mashq, never reviewed alone); round 2–3 `restyle` | weight built by rewriting one line's own stress | **another voice restates an earlier line's sweep, in its own hand and pen.** Per stretch, the outcome is drawn: *shuttle* (one contiguous back-and-forth line), *copies* (nearly on top of the stretch), or *echo* (one copy set off about 7 mm). Stretches are chosen by sustained turning × room from other ink, and cusps are penalised. |
| 3 | `dig_tremble` (contact) | rounds 3–4 tremor (v2: smooth log-normal bursts, log-sd ≈ 0.3) | a texture on every line | **tremor as a response to proximity.** Within about 5 mm of another line, the dst wavers, more the closer it comes. The other outcome is *still*: near the other line, it smooths its own turning. A `streak` param lets an outcome repeat. |
| 4 | `dig_stiffen` (contact) | round 10 `dev_rare_machine` (never reviewed) | rare stairs at a line's own salient spot | **the machine at the point of contact.** At a crossing, the line turns into a stair or a ruler for about 20 mm, then the hand resumes. Each event draws its own placement: across the crossing, arriving stiff, or leaving stiff. |
| 5 | `dig_knot` (contact) | round 11 `river_loops` (never reviewed) | oxbows tied into a line's own bends | **a lasso:** the line runs round a learned oxbow centred on the crossing, so the other line passes through the loop. *Dud, see below.* |

## Duds and what fought back

- **Knot.** On a looping hand (river meander, dance), a loop at a crossing is just one more loop, so it is invisible. On polygonal hands (meander69), it reads as clutter. Learned oxbows are crescents. To make the loop actually hold the other line, I centre it on its pole of inaccessibility (a test checks the crossing is inside it). Thin crescents then come out as little bags. One accidental good case (`try-knot`: a long blue oxbow enclosing a stretch of the black) suggests the river loop might work better *enclosing a run* than tying a knot. Not pursued.
- **Tremble at first** (amp 0.35 mm, 1.4 mm wavelength, 7 mm reach) made the whole red line look burnt and jagged. Too loud. A longer waver (5 mm) gated to near passes is the version kept.
- **Restate at first** restated kinks and curls. Measured |κ| always finds the tightest knot, and copies there turn into scribble. Penalising cusps, capping the stress and weighting by room moved it onto sweeps.
- **Pool:** a marden wet edge on a curving stretch came out as a cloud of lobes with a white slit. Ramp 1.0 × size plus the `reference` profile and spiral fill gives a round lump with a notch.
- **Coil** began as a perfect target, too mechanical. Uneven ring pace and low-order lobes (round 7's finding that wetness is lumpiness of the whole mass) turned it into a hand-wound coil.

## Dropped, and why

- **Signs as line events** (round 7): at a crossing, a sign would be thread A's `stop` with an ornament. Its lesson (attached reads as belonging) is carried by pool and knot instead.
- **measure_answer, measure_next_rehand** overlap listen's translate/imitate (thread A's porting order) and arch's passer. As relations they need a layer that is only what a relation writes (see contract).
- **poly_scatter** is placement, not something one layer does because of another (see contract).
- **Topology, path ordering, broad-nib**: no role found that wasn't decoration. Restyle lives on inside restate (`in_hand`).

## Honest read against the bar

- **Awareness, felt before named:** tremble and the echo are at the sympathy close-up's subtlety; most people won't name them. The stair and the pool are named at once, but each happens once or twice, at contacts.
- **Variety inside a technique:** every relation draws its outcome per event: wet or coil, which line pools, shuttle/copies/echo, tremble or still, stair or ruler, and where it starts.
- **Strong move at full strength:** the pool *is* arch's lesson ("one round heavy mass as the single weight"), but placed by contact. In `dig-2` it is about 11 mm across and alone.
- **Middle density, direction, elegance:** the combination sheets are the most elegant here. The single-relation pieces on Hokusai or meander69 hands are busier.
- **Would Ian keep it?** I think `dig-1`/`dig-2` (the junction weight with its quiet companions), and the echo. Stiffen is "very occasional stairs" with a reason. I'd guess "nice" for it, not a keep. Tremble may be too subtle to count. Knot, no.
- **Legibility flags:** in `dig-3`, blue's shuttle over red's loop is an oval loop with a tail and could read as an "e"/"o". Hokusai's angular black has letter-like corners (that is the base hand, not the relation). Both need a human read.

## Contract notes (what v2 couldn't express cleanly)

1. **No layer made only by relations.** A layer must have a part, lines or a composer. For a "restatement only" or "answer only" layer, the part still grows a line that is then thrown away (`keep_own=false`). An empty `part: null` layer, written by its grow relations, would fit measure_answer/rehand.
2. **Marks a relation adds take the layer's renderer.** Restatements can't get a finer pen or a different ink from their layer. A per-mark renderer hint (`meta["renderer"]`) honoured by the executor would allow it.
3. **Sheet-wide budgets across pairs.** The `*→*` expansion calls a relation once per pair. "One pool on the whole sheet" works only because the relation reads `rctx.ground` for pools already placed. A per-instance state, or a "once per score" flag, would make it explicit.
4. **Placement isn't a layer concern** (poly_scatter), only through a sealed composer, which is its old role.
