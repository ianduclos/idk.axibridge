# Round 12, thread 2: a series as the unit (prefix `series`), 2026-10-17

The question was how to make 6 to 9 sheets that are read as one set, where
the change across the set is the subject, without the set turning into a
parameter sweep.

## What I built

- **`kit/voices_series.py`**
  - **`series_travel`**: one line that travels a row of sheets. A learned
    hand still speaks every token. Each chunk is the best of K proposals, as
    in the drift line. The difference is the steering: the choice is pulled
    toward a ROUTE, a target that moves along the row as the line grows,
    instead of toward the centre of one frame. The gesture scale is one
    sheet's research-frame scale, so seven sheets hold seven sheets' worth
    of gesture, not one drawing shrunk to fit.
  - Hands hand over at authored progress values (`hand_at`).
  - `chunk` caps how far a hand runs between steering decisions. Without the
    cap, the fly hands (training strokes of 2 to 5 R) ignored the route: in
    the first try the fly never left sheets 2 and 3.
  - **`series_stretch`**: a placeholder voice for a part that takes a
    stretch of part 0's line.
- **`kit/arrangers_series.py`**
  - **`series_row`**: the recipe frame is the whole row, with no gutter in
    drawing space. Part 0's line is cut at progress values `at` into
    stretches. Each stretch goes to the next part, whose modulators and
    renderer weight it.
  - Each stretch gets an `age` channel running 0 to 1 within the stretch,
    which `trail_gallery` widens by.
  - `inks` gives one pen per part. `tuck` lets a line that emerges from a
    mass start at its rim instead of being drawn over it.
  - **`cut`, `run_series`, `run_sheet`** clip the inks to each sheet's
    rectangle. A mass on an edge is therefore split by the paper.
  - Sheet k's recipe is `{"series": recipe, "sheet": k}`, and
    `run_sheet` rebuilds that sheet exactly. `render` draws each part in
    its pen colour. `assemble` hangs the sheets on a Flexoki-100 wall.
- **`experiments/series.py`**: the two series, and `review` to rebuild the
  images below. Every built series writes `row.recipe.json` and
  `sheet-k.recipe.json` under `.local/gesture/out/series/`.
- **`tests/test_kit_series.py`**: 9 tests, all passing. They check:
  - the line reaches the first and last sheets;
  - stretch joints meet;
  - every wire ink that ends on an edge continues at the same height on the
    next sheet;
  - a sheet recipe rebuilds its sheet bit-identically;
  - output is the same under different `PYTHONHASHSEED` values;
  - a relay answer comes back as the next call with identical points.

**Format (chosen, not defaulted).** Seven A5 portrait sheets (148 × 210 mm).

- Each fits the AxiDraw bed.
- They are narrow enough that a travelling line must cross an edge about
  every 15 cm.
- Hung edge to edge they make a 1036 × 210 mm frieze, or fold as a
  leporello.

The relay uses six 180 mm squares in a row. Square sheets suit two voices
overlapping on one surface.

## The series

**Metamorphosis** (finished piece). One line, about 5.4 m long (22 R, with
R = 244 mm), seeds 4242 and 4247.

- A leaf-miner hand begins as a hairline on sheet 1 and opens into a mine
  that widens with age. The mine ends in a round chamber on the edge
  between sheets 4 and 5.
- A fly hand (`fly_fru`, in blue) walks out of the chamber, circles across
  sheet 6, and leaves the set through the top of sheet 7.
- The route's tempo gives each sheet its own character rather than another
  value of a slider: sheet 1 has only the start, sheet 2 one loose sweep,
  sheet 3 a packed circuit (the mine's walls merge as the larva doubles
  back), sheet 4 a long fall into the mass, sheet 5 a sparse rise, sheet 6
  a full cloud, and sheet 7 a single exit.
- The edges cut the line wherever it happens to be.
- The chamber lands on the 4|5 edge in 3 of 6 seeds (it is steered, not
  forced). When it does, the heaviest event of the set lives in the gap
  between two sheets.

**Relay** (contrasting idea). On sheet k, line k calls (black, nib masses)
and line k+1 answers where the call is silent (red, restated).

- On sheet k+1 that same answer returns, identical in shape, as the black
  call, and a new line answers it.
- Every line is drawn twice, in two roles, on neighbouring sheets. The
  hands pass from hokusai through bubble, marden, meander69, a river and
  the leaf miner to the fly.
- Exact identity is free: sheet k's seed is base + k, so its part 1 is
  sheet k+1's part 0.

## Honest read

I think Ian would keep Metamorphosis, or at least the mechanism.

- It is a set with one arc: a hairline, a widening mine, a mass, then a
  different hand in a different pen leaving the set. It is not a sweep,
  because no sheet is a sample of the same thing.
- It uses what he liked: the leaf miner as a mine, kinematics as a driver
  (the fly's circling becomes a pen change and a density), and a bold
  single mass.
- Single sheets stand alone well: sheet 4's fall into the half-mass, and
  sheet 7's lone exit.

Risks:

- The story can read as illustration, a miner turning into a fly.
- The mine's widening is too subtle at row scale. It is visible only at
  full size.
- The chamber's line-built fill makes a faint moiré of spokes in preview.
- A row of seven A5 sheets is small on screen (about 300 px per sheet), so
  the pair image does the real work.

The relay is the more original idea and the weaker image: red restatement
still makes elongated outlines.
