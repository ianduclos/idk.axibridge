## What the round says (lead's read)

1. **Masses that come from somewhere beat masses that are placed.** Four
   threads arrived at it independently: discharge width (3), body stillness
   (2, 4), coincidence of rewritten copies (5), the rim of a void (6). In each,
   the line → nib → mass transition is a property of the source at that point,
   not of a spatial preset — and that is what makes the transitions read as
   meaningful instead of zoned. Three agents asked for the same hook: let
   `regime_field.compose` read a per-point regime from the stroke.
2. **Shapes read as notation when they sit on a line, but they sit beside it.**
   The learned vocabulary (1) is the strongest "communicates something" image,
   yet its shapes are stamped on a baseline; in (2) and (4) the mass *is* the
   line. The obvious join: grow learned shapes at the data-born knots.
3. **Hands are the weakest axis at sheet scale — again.** Earth, dance and
   restyle sheets barely differ by hand; hybrids collapse. Hand shows in the
   single line (drift), not the sheet. The read of a sheet is decided by the
   structure and where the regimes come from.
4. **Composition: break the net.** Units, free ends, edges — the reference's
   layout is closer to a page of writing than to a drawing.
5. **Duds worth keeping:** the impossible tree drew a crown (a tree by another
   route); hummingbird trails became hatching; river bead chains repeat the
   round-6 axis dud; hybrids pooled by k-NN are just one parent; 20× mashq is
   a blob.
