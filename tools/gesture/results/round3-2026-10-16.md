## What we now know

1. **What the eye reads first: structure, then texture.** Judges cite
   figure/coherent contours first, then tremor, then junctions. Tremor is
   now modelled and moves perception; structure is best supplied
   structure-first (topology or a real drawing) and translated (restyle).
2. **Stroke-level statistics are near their floor in places and stuck in
   others.** Corners stay ~1.7–2× real in every generator (v3, v4, v5,
   lognormal, restyle) — something systematic in how segments are chosen,
   not in the representation.
3. **Hand identity survives translation** for M–H and H–I (panel). M vs I
   doesn't separate after restyle. A visual judge at crop scale can't
   identify hands at all, so it is the wrong instrument for that question.
