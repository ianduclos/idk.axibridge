# Round 15 C `arch`: archaeology, past keeps in roles they never had (2026-10-19)

**Outcome.** I built one composer, `arch`. It uses ccolour's machinery (imported, not copied) and gives
each revived family one job on the sheet. Two families clearly do a job nothing else does. The
**danced chain as the traverse** gives the sheet its direction. The **bold swell as the heavy
intruder** is the only weight on the sheet. The other two are weaker. **Limb signs as figure
flicker** read only where the line has room. **The passer**, a line that changes type where it is,
is correct when measured but subtle to the eye. The chorus entered only as a swell duet, and it
fought back. Every sheet records a `_score.Score`.

## What it does

- **The composition** comes from ccolour (`decide`, `pen_plan`, `act_core`, `colour`), with
  round-15 changes:
  - the kappa chart never enters as found matter; a river or a machine line replaces it;
  - the density dial stays in the middle (0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4, so 2 to 6 made lines), and made lines
    are shorter;
  - made lines' bold stretches are capped at 0.5 mm, so the weight belongs to the swell.
- **Focus.** Each sheet draws its families with chances 0.8, 0.75, 0.65 and 0.55, and always takes
  at least two. With three or more, the counterweight steps out.
- **danced (T2 traverse).** A drift line (matisse → shikasta → lichtenberg, chain joins) is cut into
  features. Each feature keeps its own turns step by step. A weak pull (`steer`) carries the line
  from one edge, past a point near the core's centre, to the opposite edge. The staccato tempo
  (`timing`) then adds beads at the dancer's pauses, and the line is inked as a nib. Before, a
  drift line was a blob in a box.
- **swell (T3 heavy intruder).** One short hand line (shikasta, meander69 or marden) runs through
  prosody and the swell renderer, in a box centred at the core's rim and in the intruder's pen. It
  replaces ccolour's bars, Ls and stairs. The heads are round discs (a test checks this).
  - `duet` (25%): a second swell voice in a second pen that stresses only where the first is quiet
    nearby (dev_chorus' `answer_mark`).
- **flicker (T7).** `revisit_glyph` figures (what the other limbs did during a hold) are cut out of
  the dance trail. Only figures that open in both directions and turn are kept. Each is spliced
  upright, 14 to 28 mm, into a made line at its emptiest stretch. The line stops, writes the
  figure, and goes on from the same point. The figure rises from wire to nib and falls back.
- **passer.** Two hands are cut into features. Outside the zone, the line speaks its own hand:
  accrue 3966's free walk, Cohen's meander69, or the river belt. Inside the zone, it speaks the
  core's most frequent hand, one whole feature at a time, never averaged. The zone is the
  container (T4), and the line takes the container's pen while it is inside.
- **Score.** Layers: region (the core, undrawn), found, line, mask (the found_play cover),
  container, counter, traverse, intruder, passer, flicker. Relations:
  - `cross`: the traverse crosses the core region;
  - `intrude`: the swell enters at the core's rim;
  - `surface`: a figure surfaces from its host line, at a point and an arc-length position;
  - `translate`: the passer speaks the core's hand, over arc-length ranges;
  - `contain`: the container is the passer's zone;
  - `answer`: the swell duet;
  - ccolour's colour events, mapped to `ride`, `echo`, `spill` and `contact`.

  New kinds are `cross`, `intrude`, `surface` and `contain`. Decisions hold the frame, density,
  core, blank, families, act order and pen plan.

## The duds and what fought back

- **First try, all four families in every sheet, on ccolour's own dial** (working images
  `.local/gesture/out/arch/debug-*`).
  - The swell was small and drowned among found_play's bold 1.0–1.4 mm stretches.
  - The figures drowned in the tangle.
  - The passer's change of hand happened inside the core, in the core's pen, so it was invisible.
  - The sheets read as ccolour with more stuff in them: the museum risk. Three fixes: bold capped,
    families sampled per sheet, the passer's zone moved to the container.
- **Flicker fights back.** Figures placed near the core's rim were invisible. They now go where a
  line has room, but several still read as heavy little scribbles rather than signs. The orange
  "flame" in 15005 is the good case. Limb signs worked as a whole drawing (one body); as single
  figures they lose the body that made them a family.
- **The chorus (swell duet) fights back.** Two swells in two pens read as two intruders, not as
  call and response. At this scale the listening is not visible. I left it at 25% rather than
  making it a role.
- **The passer is subtle.** In `arch-5-roles` you can see its free hand wiggle outside and the
  smoother core hand inside the container, in the container's pen. In a full sheet it takes a
  close look. That may suit Ian's "shouldn't be obvious", or it may go unnoticed.
- **Strips (300 × 125)** can crowd the core at one end; 15004 drew no traverse and reads weak.

## Honest read against the bar

- **Direction:** yes, where the danced chain is present (15002, 15006, 15008, and 15010 outside
  the population). It is the best revival: a long line with beads that leaves the core, ties the
  layers together and reaches an edge. It does a job nothing else in the kit does.
- **Middle density:** mostly. Some cores are still a found_play knot (15003). ccolour's weak point,
  one core act, is inherited.
- **One trick for the eye:** only partly. The intended trick is the passer changing hand and pen
  inside the container. It is too quiet to count as the sheet's trick. The 4507-like void appears
  only when found_play's cover fires (15008).
- **Would Ian keep it?** Probably as moves, not as sheets: the danced traverse and the swell
  intruder as bench layers. Of the sheets, 15002 and 15006 are the closest. Flicker and the duet I
  would not push in this form. The lead ranked densest first twice; my three picks are the
  population's mid-density sheets with the clearest single line.
- **Flag (legibility):** some flicker figures make letter-like strokes (a black "K"/"X" in 15005).
  They are not legible, but they need a human read before any keep.
- **Surprise:** 15010 (a 300 × 125 strip, working images only). A black danced chain spans the
  whole strip, and a small blue swell sits inside the container. It is the most elegant sheet I
  rendered. It is outside the population, so it is not in the review set.

## Files

- `tools/gesture/gesture/kit/arrangers_arch.py`: arranger `arch` and `plan_arch`, `steer`,
  `tokens`, `turns`, `ray_exit`, the acts (`act_danced`, `act_swell`, `act_passer` +
  `colour_passer`, `figures` + `act_flicker`), `build_score`, `recipe`, `render`.
- `tools/gesture/gesture/kit/renderers_arch.py`: renderer `arch_ink` (wire / nib / swell per mark,
  pen colour in `Ink.meta`).
- `tools/gesture/experiments/arch.py`: `one`, `pop` and `review` (rebuilds the 5 images). Working
  images go to `.local/gesture/out/arch/`, with `.recipe.json`, `.score.json` and `.log.json`
  beside each.
- `tools/gesture/experiments/arch_debug.py`: a role-coloured view of a sheet.
- `tools/gesture/tests/test_kit_arch.py`: 12 tests. They cover:
  - registration;
  - roles, with the chart retired, middle density, bold capped and every family present;
  - determinism, in process and across processes with `PYTHONHASHSEED`;
  - a complete score (relations name real layers, no self-relations);
  - inks inside the paper, in real pens;
  - only the swell is heavy, and its heads are round;
  - flicker figures leave and return to their host, rising from wire to nib;
  - the passer changes pen only in its zone, as one contiguous line;
  - `steer` reaches its goal by way of the waypoint;
  - an unknown family is refused.

  They pass, and `test_kit.py` passes (53 in all).

## Proposed core changes (not made)

- ccolour's `colour()` events record the lender only as prose (`why`), so `build_score` parses it.
  An explicit `src` object key per event would make the score exact.
- The `Score` has no field for a layer's undrawn zone geometry. I recorded the core as a `region`
  layer with `technique: "none"`. The bench may want a first-class role for undrawn regions.
