## Reading across the three

1. **Metamorphosis works best where it is structural.** A trunk becomes a
   mass, a line pools into a dot, a stem flares into a vessel. It looks
   glued-on when it is periodic: capsule beads, regular drips.
2. **Signs should grow out of lines, not be stamped on them.** Next: the
   placement grammar chooses *where*; regime operators make the sign *from
   the line* (terminal drops, beads, eyes as a line's own events).
3. **The nib at 0.35 mm pen reads as a hollow pipe** below r ≈ 1.2. Options:
   denser inset fill earlier, a narrower nib-outline gap, or several pens.
   This is the open thickness question again; Ian's call.
4. Missing compared with the sense of the reference: irregular wet mass
   edges, variety in masses, script-like zones that aren't confetti.
