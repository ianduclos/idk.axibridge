# Bursty tremor (tremor v2) — 2026-10-16

Question: the round-3 tremor layer moved the blind judge (v4 spotted 29/30
without it, 20/30 with it), but its texture is stationary. On the sheets,
real lines waver in bursts and calm stretches, with occasional kinks, at
longer wavelengths. Is that intermittency measurable? Can a model reproduce
it?

Code:
- `gesture/tremor.py`, v2 sections: `burst_stats`, `fit_regime`,
  `synth_regime`, `synth_kinks`. `apply_tremor(..., mode="v1")` keeps the
  round-3 layer.
- `experiments/tremor_v2_study.py`, stages `floor burst model eval pairs
  sheets`.
- `tests/test_tremor.py`: 14 tests in 3 s.

Raw outputs are in `.local/gesture/out/tremor_v2/` (`burst.json`/`.log`,
`models/*.json` with `summary.json`, `eval.json`/`.log`, `sheets/`). The v1
model files in `out/tremor/models/` are untouched, so `composite.py` still
gets v1.

## 1. Measurement

**Residual.** As in round 3: resampled at 0.25 w, P smoothed at 0.3 w, and
the centreline C twiced. It is measured in two bands:

| band | C smoothing | wavelengths kept |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3–2 w | 2 w | λ ≲ 10 w |
| 0.3–4 w | 4 w | λ ≲ 25 w |

**Floor.** A new floor was re-extracted at the 4 w band, from the same 45
scans with the ground truth smoothed at 8 w. The ground truth itself leaks
0.04–0.06 w into the 4 w band, so that band's floor is mostly leakage, not
extraction.

**Envelope.** e(s) is the log of the local rms (Gaussian window σ = 2 w,
about 5 w wide), computed within corner-free runs ≥ 8 w, skipping 2 w at
the ends.

**Null.** Every run is compared with its own **phase-randomised surrogate**.
The surrogate has the same spectrum and the same per-stroke level, but is
stationary within the run. Burstiness = what real has beyond the surrogate.

**κ-normalised.** The set's own rms-by-|κ| profile is divided out first, so
the curvature dependence (already in v1) does not count as bursts.

**Burst statistics** (all on the κ-normalised envelope):

| # | statistic | definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lsdW` | within-stroke sd of e(s) |
| 2 | exc | √(lsdW² − lsdW_surr²) |
| 3 | envL | correlation length of e(s) |
| 4 | shaky / calm dwell | lengths of the runs where e(s) is above / below median + log 1.5 |
| 5 | kinks | excursions with \|r\| > 3.5 rms, per 100 w |
| 6 | d2-kinks | excursions with \|Δ²r\| > 5 rms(Δ²r), i.e. sudden direction changes, per 100 w |

What the 0.3–4 w band adds (`burst.log`): the same picture. exc is 0.36–0.38
for real against 0.18–0.32 for v4 + v1.

1. **Real tremor is non-stationary, and twice as much as v1.**
   - The within-stroke envelope varies beyond a stationary process by a
     log-sd of ≈ 0.29–0.30 in every hand and medium (litho 0.24). v1 reaches
     only 0.07–0.15.
   - The envelope stays correlated a little longer than the surrogate:
     4.6–5.9 w against 4.1–4.5.
2. **It is continuous, not two-state.**
   - The log-envelope is unimodal (ΔBIC ≈ 0).
   - Dwell lengths above and below a 1.5× threshold are the same as the
     surrogate's.
   - "Calm and shaky stretches" is a log-normal amplitude modulation on a
     ≈ 4–8 w scale, plus (Ingres) occasional very calm pockets. It is not a
     switch.
3. **Kinks.**
   - Sudden direction changes (d2-kinks) run 1.2–2.1× the stationary rate.
     Hokusai ×2.1, Matisse ×1.8, Ingres ×1.2.
   - Large excursions are 1.2–1.8×, but they cluster at junctions:
     Hokusai 65 % of kinks within 3 w of another stroke against 37 % of
     samples, Ingres 36 % vs 19 %.
   - Away from junctions, the Hokusai excursion rate equals the surrogate's.
4. **Triggers.**
   - *Curvature:* handled by κ-normalisation, already in v1.
   - *Junctions:* the envelope rises sharply within 2 w of another stroke
     (+0.4–0.6 log). **But the re-extracted floor shows the same or a
     larger rise** (Matisse floor +0.68 vs real +0.48 at < 2 w, 4 w band).
     **Junction bursts are an extraction artefact, not modelled.**
   - *Ends:* +0.1–0.15 log within 5 w, also matched by the floor. v1's end
     gain already covers it.
   - *Stroke length:* longer strokes are calmer (ρ = −0.19 Matisse, −0.12
     Ingres, 0 Hokusai), as in round 3; the v1 per-stroke gain absorbs it.
   - Excluding samples within 5 w of a junction lowers the fitted σ from
     0.4 to 0.3. It does not remove the burstiness.
5. **Medium (Matisse).**
   - Lithograph is *less* bursty than etching: exc 0.24 vs 0.30, d2-kinks
     0.43 vs 1.21 per 100 w, no junction concentration of kinks.
   - Its κ dependence is weaker after normalisation (ρ 0.07 vs 0.20).
   - Reading: crayon grain adds stationary texture that dilutes the bursts;
     the bursty part looks like hand. Etching's thin line (2.6 px) also
     makes junction glitches likelier.
6. **The 4 w band reveals a real spectral gap.** v4 + v1 lacks energy at
   λ 8–16 w:

   | artist | real (w) | v4 + v1 (w) |
   |---|---|---|
   | Matisse | 0.074 | 0.054 |
   | Hokusai | 0.108 | 0.091 |
   | Ingres | 0.104 | 0.072 |

   This is the longer-wavelength wavering seen on the sheets.

## 2. Model (tremor v2)

The residual along s is built from four parts:

  r(s) = [base(s) · env_v1(κ, end) · g_stroke · g_regime(s) + kinks(s)] · amount

1. **base.** Stationary coloured noise, now fitted over the 0.3–4 w band.
   - PSD = real − floor − **v4's own PSD in that band**. It supplies only what
     the generator lacks, mostly λ 8–20 w.
   - The measurement band-pass is deconvolved (power gain ≤ 5).
   - It is calibrated round trip on v4 drawings to the missing variance.
   - It is generator-specific, by construction.
2. **g_regime** (`synth_regime`, kind `lognormal`). g = exp(σh − σ²), where h
   is a unit Gaussian process with a Gaussian ACF of scale `corr_w`, so
   E[g²] = 1. A `markov2` kind (two states, geometric dwells, eased edges)
   exists. It fits real slightly worse than lognormal (loss 0.008–0.028 vs
   0.002–0.006), as the bimodality test predicted.
3. **kinks** (`synth_kinks`). Poisson positions, random sign, log-normal
   amplitude, cusp shape exp(−|s − s0|/0.75 w).
4. **Fitting** (`fit_regime`) is by simulation.
   - Every real κ-normalised run is replaced by its phase surrogate × a
     candidate regime (+ kinks).
   - It is measured exactly like real, and the candidate closest on
     (lsdW, envL), then (d2-kinks, kurtosis), is chosen.
   - Synthesis is from noise only. Nothing is resampled from real strokes.
5. **env_v1 and g_stroke** are kept from the v1 model.
6. **Structure safety** is unchanged: ends are pinned and contacts
   re-snapped, through the same code path.

## 3. Evaluation

Real long-stroke drawings (31 / 27 / 34) were compared with 16 v4 drawings
per artist, plus v1 or v2 at amount 1. Features per drawing:
- v1 tremor statistics;
- burst statistics at both bands, κ-normalised;
- 4 w band powers.

AUC is 5×5-fold logistic regression; the per-feature column is mean
|AUC − 0.5|.

| # | artist | feature set | real vs v4+v1 | real vs v4+v2 | v4+v1 vs v4+v2 | per-feature real/v1 → real/v2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Matisse | burst only | 0.96 | 0.94 | 0.98 | 0.21 → **0.24** |
| 2 | Hokusai | burst only | 0.96 | **0.79** | 1.00 | 0.21 → **0.10** |
| 3 | Ingres | burst only | 0.99 | **0.88** | 0.94 | 0.27 → **0.16** |
| 4 | Matisse / Hokusai / Ingres | v1 tremor stats only | 0.93 / 0.92 / 1.00 | 0.90 / 0.79 / 0.86 | — | — |
| 5 | Matisse | panel σ ≥ 7 w | 0.84 | 0.85 | 0.07\* | 0.095 → 0.094 |
| 6 | Hokusai | panel σ ≥ 7 w | 0.82 | 0.88 | 0.12\* | 0.109 → 0.110 |
| 7 | Ingres | panel σ ≥ 7 w | 0.92 | 0.96 | 0.14\* | 0.129 → 0.136 |

\* Below 0.5 means v1 and v2 are indistinguishable at 7 w. The per-feature
|AUC − 0.5| for v1 vs v2 there is 0.013–0.023.

1. **Hokusai and Ingres improve clearly.** Single features that were off
   under v1 land at the real median under v2:

   | feature | artist | real vs v4+v1 AUC | real vs v4+v2 AUC | real / v1 / v2 median |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | env lsdW | Hokusai | 0.00 | 0.40 | 0.41 / 0.32 / 0.40 |
   | envL | Hokusai | 0.08 | 0.40 | — |
   | d2-kinks | Hokusai | 0.14 | 0.56 | — |
   | λ 8–16 w | Ingres | 0.00 | 0.31 | 0.104 / 0.072 / 0.096 |
   | corr length | Ingres | 0.02 | 0.66 | — |

   The multivariate classifiers drop by 0.1–0.17 but stay well above
   chance.
2. **Matisse does not improve; it overshoots.**
   - v2 makes the envelope too variable: lsdW 0.45 vs real 0.39.
   - It doubles the d2-kink rate: 1.01 vs 0.53 per 100 w.
   - Kurtosis rises further: 7.6 vs 4.4, and v1 was already high at 6.7.
   - Cause: the fit uses pooled runs, which a few dense etching sheets
     dominate. Their per-drawing medians are calmer. The Matisse kinks
     (2.0 per 100 w) are the likely culprit.
   - Fix next time: fit targets as per-drawing medians, or use
     `matisse_etching` (no kinks) as the Matisse default.
3. **The 7 w standard panel is unaffected.** v1 and v2 are
   indistinguishable at 7 w (AUC 0.07–0.14, per-feature 0.01–0.02).
   - Real vs v4+v2 rises by +0.01 / +0.06 / +0.03 over v4+v1. That is
     within CV noise at n = 16.
   - It is consistent with the added λ 8–20 w energy touching the panel's
     finest scale. Watch it, but don't act on it yet.

## 5. Sheets: `tremor-v2-{matisse,hokusai,ingres}.jpg`

Columns: real | v4 + v1 ×1 | v4 + v2 ×1 | v4 + v1 ×2 | v4 + v2 ×2. The same
v4 drawing and the same seed run throughout. The top row shows whole
drawings; the bottom row a 1:1 detail. Render: 1200 px, 1.5-px line, 1-px
smoothing.

1. **At ×1 the two are hard to tell apart** at sheet scale. v2's lines carry
   slightly longer, softer undulation; v1's carry a finer, even fuzz.
2. **At ×2 the difference is clear.**
   - v1 is the round-3 "coastline": every line crinkles at the same rate.
     Hokusai's central loop is uniformly knobbly all the way round.
   - Under v2 the same loop is smooth along one side and wavers along the
     other. Long contours alternate quieter and livelier stretches, and the
     wobble is longer-wavelength.
   - That is closer to the real column: Hokusai's brush contour meanders
     at long wavelength with a few kinks; Ingres lines are mostly calm with
     the occasional local wobble.
3. **It is still not a hand.**
   - v2's bursts are short (≈ 4 w correlation), so "stretches" are a few
     line widths, not whole passages.
   - Some v2 lines show a small cluster of jogs, where a burst and a kink
     coincide. That reads as a glitch more than a hesitation.
   - The real Hokusai meander has more amplitude at λ > 20 w. That is
     structure beyond any tremor band.
4. **Verdict.** v2 reads *less like a filter* than v1. It reads as
   uneven-pressure line rather than uniform noise, but it is subtle at ×1.
   On Matisse it is a little too busy, as the statistics said.

## 7. Recommendation

1. **Make v2 the default for Hokusai and Ingres.** Keep v1 (or
   `matisse_etching` v2, which has no kinks) for Matisse until the fit uses
   per-drawing targets.
2. **Run the 89 judge pairs.** The paired v1/v2 design answers whether the
   bursts move the eye.
3. **Don't model junction bursts.** The extraction floor produces them.
4. **If a hand-scale "passage" effect is wanted** (calm for 30 w, then
   agitated), it is not in these data at the 0.3–4 w band. It would have to
   come from the grammar or composition, not from tremor.

## Blind judge on the v1/v2 pairs (run by the lead after this report)

Sonnet judge, images only, key outside the repo; 89 pairs, 1 px smoothing.

| # | condition | judge correct | p |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | real vs v4 + tremor v1 | 14/30 = 0.47 | .86 |
| 2 | real vs v4 + tremor v2 | 14/30 = 0.47 | .86 |
| 3 | calibration: real vs real with its tremor swapped for v2 | 14/29 = 0.48 | 1.0 |

- **With tremor, the judge is at chance** (round 3 had 20/30 with v1 and
  29/30 with no tremor). Treat this as consistent with round 3: tremor
  removes the judge's main perceptual tell. It is not a fresh no-tremor
  comparison, because this run had no no-tremor condition.
- **v1 vs v2 cannot be compared from this run.** The two conditions used the
  same drawings and crop windows. The judge noticed the repeats and answered
  them consistently, so the per-artist numbers are *identical* by
  construction. Design lesson: matched conditions must go to separate judges
  (or separate sessions) so that consistency can't link them.
- The judge's cues: content and structure first; texture "visible but a weak,
  unreliable secondary cue"; it suspected stroke-end blobs from tracing.
