# Perceived lines: merging extracted fragments into lines as drawn (2026-10-16)

Files: `gesture/perceive.py`, `tests/test_perceive.py` (7 tests, ~1.6 s),
`experiments/perceive_study.py` (`synth`, `corpus`, `overlays`, `prepare`,
`calibrate`, `test1`, `memo`, `sheets`).
Raw outputs: `.local/gesture/out/perceive/` (synth.json, corpus.json, overlays/,
logs), perceived drawings in `.local/gesture/out/perceived/{artist}/`, the
perceived grammar and its test 1 / memo in `.local/gesture/out/grammar_perceived/`,
discrimination in `.local/gesture/out/discriminate/perceived-v4-standard/`.
Sheets kept here: `perceive-overlays.jpg`, `perceive-generated.jpg`.

**Verdict.**

- `perceive()` works as a measurement view. On synthetic ground truth it
  makes merges at precision **0.90–0.92** and recall **0.67–0.79**,
  depending on the vertex rule. The overlays look right where it merges:
  Ingres's head contour becomes one line, turtle shells close up, and hatching
  is left alone.
- **It does not fix length or corners at the source.**
  - Generated `len_q10` stays at 0.70–0.88 × real.
  - Corner excess stays: the generator is 1.1–1.5 × the perceived real rate,
    and 1.5–1.7 × the original real rate.
  - Test 1 does not improve.
  - Two causes:
    1. `len_q10` of the long subset is pinned by the 40 w selection floor and
       the R normalisation. Merging adds more lines that just clear the floor.
    2. Merging puts the joins' corners into the real data. The training set
       gets more corners, not fewer.
- **Recommendation:** do not make perceived lines the default *training* view
  for the token grammar. Keep `perceive` as a measurement and composition
  layer. That means the topology chain view (topology next step 2), skeleton
  lines for structure-first generation, and the `len_ink` share of long lines.

## 1. Method

`perceive(drawing, **params) -> Drawing` works on strokes only, with no
raster.

1. **Candidates are end-to-end only.**
   - Every pair of open ends within `gap_w` = 12 line widths is a candidate.
   - An end on another line's interior (a T) has no end partner there. So **a
     stem can never merge into its host**, by construction.
2. **Decisions.** w is the larger median width of the two strokes. End
   tangents are outward chords over 4 w.
   - **join**: the ends touch (≤ 1.5 w) and the deflection is ≤ 75° (0 means
     straight on). This matches the extractor, which already returns a bare
     V as one stroke.
     - **Vertex rule.** If a third line's end or interior is within touch
       distance, only ≤ 35° is allowed. Two lines that end at one point of a
       third line form a vertex, not a corner.
   - **near / gap**: the two ends are apart.
     - Both ends must aim across the gap, within 35° at touch distance,
       shrinking linearly to 12° at 12 w.
     - The total turn must be ≤ 45°.
     - The gap must be ≤ the shorter stroke's length.
     - A gap ≤ 4 w counts as *near*. A longer one counts as *gap* (faint-ink
       breaks).
     - A lateral offset fails the aim test, which rules out parallel
       neighbours and staggered double contours.
   - **cross**: a near/gap merge whose bridge crosses another line. This
     completes an X that the extractor failed to pair.
   - **close**: a chain's two free ends meet. The result is a closed stroke.
3. **Matching.**
   - Candidates are taken greedily by cost, and each end is used at most once.
   - **Ambiguity guard:** a candidate is skipped if either end has another
     acceptable free partner within 1.3 × its cost. A symmetric Y, for
     example, stays unmerged.
4. **Geometry and provenance.**
   - A Hermite bridge joins the two strokes; widths are interpolated across it.
   - Stroke meta records `fragments` ([input index, reversed]), `joins`
     (kind, gap_w, deflection, aim) and `bridges` (point ranges).
   - Drawing meta `perceive` records the parameters and counts.
5. **Purity and determinism** are tested.

**X crossings, checked.** The extractor already pairs clean X crossings
(`synth.scene_x_crossing`: 2 strokes). `cross` merges catch the rest.

## 2. Synthetic ground truth

Setup: 40 scenes (`scene_random` / `scene_matisse_like`, w = 3 clean and w = 5
noisy, 900 px). Breaks were added on purpose:

- clean gaps of 2–8 w with a ±0.5 w lateral re-placement;
- faint-ink gaps of 4–12 w at 15 % darkness;
- 2–5 w breaks at crossings;
- 1–2 T stems per scene;
- one 50–75° corner with a spur into the V.

Each scene was rendered, run through `extract_hybrid` and then `perceive`. A
merge is correct when both fragments belong to the same true stroke (majority
owner). Recall = correct merges ÷ needed joins, where needed joins = Σ
(fragments per true stroke − 1).

| # | variant | merges | precision | recall | continuity before → after | fragments / true stroke | false merges with a T stem |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | T-interior vertex rule only | 171 | **.918** | **.793** | .734 → .903 | 1.59 → 1.15 | 5 |
| P2 | **default**: + third-end vertex rule | 147 | .905 | .672 | .734 → .877 | 1.59 → 1.21 | 5 |

Per kind (P1):

- gap: precision .91 (139 merges);
- join: .96 (25);
- cross: .86 (7).

P2 loses the corner-with-spur joins (the spur's end makes the apex a vertex)
and keeps the Y/T safety. Ink precision is unchanged (.978).

Where the false merges come from:

- **5 merges join a T stem to a host whose own break falls at the stem's
  foot.** This is a constructed coincidence, but a plausible one.
- **9 merges join two different random strokes that happen to end aligned.**
  Many sit in degenerate `scene_random` coincidences.

## 7. Recommendation

1. **Not the default training view for the token grammar.**
   - Learning from perceived lines fixes neither `len_q10` nor corners, and
     it puts join corners into the hand.
   - The length tell is a property of the long-subset selection and the R
     normalisation. That is worth a separate look: compare at a common floor,
     or measure `len_q10` on all strokes ≥ 40 w in px ÷ w.
2. **Use it where lines-as-seen matter.**
   - The topology chain view (topology next step 2): near ends become joins,
     and isolated-stroke counts drop.
   - Skeleton targets for structure-first generation: one skeleton line per
     perceived line, redrawn by restyle.
   - Contiguous-line statistics: the `len_ink` share in long lines (C3).
   - A natural extension: learn the grammar per fragment, but learn the
     *composition* (which fragments chain, and at what join angle) from the
     perceived joins in `meta["joins"]`. That is exactly the join-deflection
     data the next grammar needs (topology §3 V1–V2).
3. **Two open threshold choices:**
   - the third-end vertex rule (P1 vs P2), a trade between corner recall and Y
     safety;
   - `gap_w` = 12 against faint pencil, where Matisse misses are still
     visible.
