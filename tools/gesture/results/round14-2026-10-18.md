## Per thread

### c1 rzoom (committed 59bccfd)
- Mechanism works: dense far twists open into large loops and close again, one line, real bends. Camera window = dud (diagram).
- My read: the best passages are mid zoom (~3–10x): bends still read as river at a visible size. Above ~16x they become smooth balloons, generic hand loops; the river character goes. Agrees with agent.
- Population: schedules differ mostly in where the balloons fall; at sheet level they look alike (big black loops + blue thin twists). Not "different every time" as compositions, but it's a voice, not a composer.
- Pick: the piece (push), for the passage top-right where twists grow through 3 sizes. Suggest cap zoom ~10–16 and use as found matter in b.

### c2 rnear (committed)
- Reads more like a river than c1: peaks are lower, so most of the strip stays in the 3–10x band where bends keep their character. Strongest: population A (stem only) and H (never close, peak 10): the river breathes rather than balloons.
- Handover to the hands is invisible in black (agent agrees); only the colour map shows it. The cut-off at the nearest approach (E) is the one second-regime event, mild.
- Flaw: at a peak one bend becomes a long plain arc (the near-vertical drop in the piece, the long strokes in A/G); reads as a dead stroke, not a river close up.
- Combined verdict for (c): keep the magnified stem with a peak around 8–12x; drop the hand ladder; the zooming river is found matter for (b).
