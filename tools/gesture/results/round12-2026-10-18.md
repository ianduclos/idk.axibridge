## Lead's read (written before Ian's notes)

1. **Drivers is the pathway that answered.** The twisting ribbon is new in the
   bank and the honest test holds: hide the drivers and the rhythm is still
   there; shuffle the fly and it goes flat. With its drum-record plate it is
   also the drawing-beside-its-measurement Ian keeps choosing.
2. **Oehlen conduction is the strongest single sheet** — bold, few, pushed to
   one side, the conductor sitting small beside its performance. Its risk is
   style: it looks like the 2016 charcoals.
3. **Found ground is the most Oehlen-like as a procedure** and the river ride is
   the best moment; the cut cover is too subtle at sheet size.
4. **The series works as a unit**: the paper edge splitting the chamber is a
   real event no single sheet has. It may read as illustration (miner turns
   into fly).
5. **Stains are a new mass vocabulary**, not contour maps, but the frilly big
   rings are near roses; Ian may find them decorative.
6. **Intuition** is the least like the project and the most uneven: the knot
   with its hole is alive, the burst is cartoonish, the pixel staircase quotes
   Oehlen too directly.
7. **Revisit** half-answers "less obvious shapes that communicate"; the floor
   glyphs need his eye for letters.
8. **Outsider**: the chosen portrait is a tasteful one-line drawing — close to a
   familiar genre; the denser alternative is more alive. A second model did not
   find a pathway the lead's agents lacked, but it did avoid every house habit.
