## Ian's two questions, answered

- **Contagion: "was this all algorithmic?"** Yes. One `dance_thrusting` gesture
  from the library runs through the `contagion` arranger over found ground's
  plate and the far river. Ride, catch, carry and shed are all rules in code;
  nothing is hand-placed. The one human-like choice was the agent picking seed
  5302 out of 168 renders by reading the infection maps, not by score.
- **Stages: "real river meanders or a statistical profile?"** Real ones. The
  bends that enter the line are the frozen far-view GRWL stem's own bends,
  taken from past the end of the pasted fragment. Contagion is the opposite
  case: its carried river is generated fresh from the river's measured
  statistics (swing and half-bend length), so it is a statistical profile. That
  is why it looks more even.
