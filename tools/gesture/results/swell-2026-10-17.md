# Round 11, exploration 2: swelling into mass (2026-10-17)

A line can swell smoothly into a mass and still stay round. The trick is to keep the slow part of the swell at line width and let only the head bloom. The bloom must meet a round head at a slope below 1. `drop`, `press` and `lens` do this. Their transitions break about five times less sharply than a round-10 bead (median slope break 0.29 against 1.44), and their masses are as round (median elongation 1.11–1.14, max ≤ 1.26). The naive smooth swell (`bell`) turns into a sausage once it is longer than about 0.6 head diameters.

Run it with `.local/gesture/.venv/bin/python -m tools.gesture.experiments.swell [profiles closeup threshold dots bold combo dotshapes]`. A full run takes about 90 s. Every tile was made with `kit.run` + `kit.preview`, and its recipe sits beside it in `.local/gesture/out/swell/`. Each sheet's cell recipes are in `sheet-*.recipe.json`, and all the numbers are in `numbers.json`.

## Modules (`tools/gesture/gesture/kit/swell_ink.py`)

| kind | id | what |
|---|---|---|
| renderer | `swell` | The line is inked as a sweep of discs whose radius r(s) grows smoothly from the pen to the mass and back. It has eight profiles: `bell`, `lens`, `drop`, `press`, `turn`, `momentum`, `sag`, `necklace`. The head sits where `dev_beads` would put its bead, with the same diameter. `line: none` makes a ghost. |
| renderer | `swell_dots` | Dots placed by a visible rule: `inflection`, `turns`, `beat`, `count`, `decel`, `fullstop`, `pauses`. Sizes come from a pattern of L/m/s with `.` for a rest. Shapes come per size: `disc`, `ring`, `split`, `comma`, `wound`. `line: swell` adds swells too. |
| arranger | `swell_place` | Each part goes in its own authored box (fractions of the frame, overlap allowed), with `inks` for colour previews. |

Fills are line-built. Each component gets its outline ring plus one inset spiral; a split recurses into one path per child. Disc dots are one true Archimedean spiral. The `wound` dot is the line itself winding out and back in, spliced into the line, so line and dot are one path.

## 1. Profiles: same line, same stresses, same head sizes (`swell-profiles.jpg`, `swell-closeup.jpg`)

The line is the round-10 drift (matisse > shikasta > lichtenberg, chain, seed 7000) with segment stresses. `margin` is pinned to 5.25 mm, the round-10 bead reach. Without the pin, each renderer's reach changes the margin, the line is fitted at a different scale, and prosody picks different stresses. My first sheet compared different lines this way. With the pin, the gather numbers reproduce round 10 exactly.

Two measures:

- **Mass elongation.** This is round 10's measure: open the ink with a 1.6 mm disc and take √(λmax/λmin) per blob. A disc scores 1.
- **Smoothness.** This uses ρ(s), the inscribed half-thickness: the distance from the centreline to the ink's edge, in mm per mm. It is measured on each stress's flanks, below 70 % of ρ's max, because every round mass has an apex that is not a transition.
  - "slope" is max |dρ/ds|.
  - "break" is max |Δ dρ/ds| over 1 mm. It shows a C1 break: a bead meeting the line breaks by about 1–2.

I first tried the width across the normal. It spiked wherever a head sat on a corner, which is most heads, so I dropped it.

Momentum throws the head past the corner, and the line leaves from its side; once it curls into a C with a pinhole. Necklace makes beads on smooth necks. It reads well, but its lobes fuse at corners (max 2.39).

## 2. Where smooth tips into sausage (`swell-threshold.jpg`)

| setting | elongation median / max |
|---|---|
| bell length 0.35 / 0.6 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.2 / 3.2 D | 1.07 / 1.09 · 1.19 / 1.21 · **1.42 / 1.49** · 1.70 / 1.74 · 1.70 / 2.25 · 1.77 / 2.95 |
| drop k_head 0.15 / 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.75 / 0.95 | **1.47 / 2.46** · 1.26 / 1.92 · 1.19 / 1.50 · 1.14 / 1.26 · 1.10 / 1.17 |
| drop tail_w 1.0 / 2.4 / 3.6 / 5.0 mm | 1.14–1.15 / 1.23–1.26 (insensitive) |

- **Bell:** round up to 0.6 D, a sausage from 1.0 D.
- **Drop:** round while the bloom meets the head at slope ≥ 0.5; below 0.3 it is a teardrop sausage.
- **tail_w:** the tail's width barely matters, because the exponential tail is short where it is wide.

The rule that holds: **let the slow part of the swell happen at line widths, and make the transition into the mass itself fast but tangent.**

## 3. Deliberate dots (`swell-dots.jpg`, `swell-dotshapes.jpg`)

Each tile has one rule, a size vocabulary, and a shape per size. Rules that read at a glance:

- `beat`: a dot every 16 mm, L s s, a waltz.
- `decel`: dots close up and grow toward each hand switch, like a slowing pen.
- `fullstop`: each phrase ends in a dot, the pen lifts, and the line resumes.

`count` (groups of 1, 2, 3 at the strongest events) reads only if you look for it. `pauses` (Staccato dwell peaks: loud = split, quiet = small disc) gave few dots on this line.

The shapes are the new part. The close-up at 10 px/mm shows:

- `ring`: the line shows through the hole.
- `split`: the line runs through the slit.
- `comma`: a dot with a curling tail.
- `wound`: the line winds out and back in, as one path with the line.

Dots here are disc-round (elongation 1.05–1.14). The `count` max of 2.32 comes from touching dots inside a group.

## 4. Bold gestures (`swell-bold.jpg`)

One hand at length 0.8–1.0 fills the sheet or a third of it, with heads of 15–24 mm and pens of 0.55–0.7 mm.

- (a) shikasta: two drops on one sweep and a big open loop.
- (b) three hands in authored thirds: hokusai drop, roadrunner press, matisse momentum.
- (c) meander69: lands heavy at its loudest turn and lifts across.
- (d) marden: round masses on its tightest turns.

The transitions are the smoothest in the round: break 0.14–0.20 on (a) and (c), because the gesture's own scale gives the swell room.

The honest number: at the fixed 1.6 mm opening, (a) to (c) score 1.42–1.64 median. Their 2–3 mm tails survive the opening. With the opening scaled to 0.2 × head size, they score 1.16–1.27 median and 1.33–1.53 max. They read as comets and drops, not capsules, but they are the most elongated thing here. (d) is 1.10–1.12 either way.

## What failed

- `bell` is the sausage. Any symmetric smooth swell with a flat top longer than about 0.6 D elongates (1.42 at 1 D, 1.77 / 2.95 at 3.2 D).
- `necklace` (max 2.39), `momentum` (max 1.90) and `sag` (max 1.63) tip into elongated blobs at corners, where lobes or offset discs fuse. Their medians stay at 1.20–1.23.
- `drop` and `press` are mirror images. In a still image you cannot tell travel direction, so they differ only in which side of the event the long tail falls.
- `turn` took three versions:
  - Curvature normalised inside its window made a gentle bend a 28 mm sausage (caught by a test).
  - Absolute curvature merged every chain corner into one mass (max 2.65).
  - The kept version is relative in shape (^3) with its amplitude gated by how tight the turn is (1.13 / 1.22).
- Prosody's event detector hears inflections at a fixed ~5 mm scale and found almost none on big smooth hands, so `swell_dots` has its own scale-aware detector (`scale`).
- `pauses` on a Staccato drift: emphasis tops out at 0.42, so "loud" had to become relative to the line's own loudest pause. It still yields few dots.
- `mass_shapes` misses dots under 3.2 mm, so the ring, wound and inflection tiles score 0 masses. That is a measure limit, not a result.
- (b) in bold: the three thirds overlap and read busier than the single gestures.

## Proposed core changes (not made)

1. A shared kit helper for line-built fills that handles splits and holes (`swell_ink.line_fill` does this). `dev_beads.solid` keeps only the largest piece when an inset splits, and fills only the outer half of an annulus.
2. `mass_shapes` and the ρ(s) smoothness measure as a kit metric module, with an optional scale-relative opening (0.2 × head size). The fixed 1.6 mm opening penalises bold pieces for their tails.
3. Comparisons across renderers need a pinned `margin`. Otherwise each renderer's reach refits the line and changes its prosody. Either document this in the kit README or let `margin` default to the voice's reach only.
4. `render_image` should honour a per-part ink (repeats round 10's proposal). Here `swell_place` carries `inks`, and the experiment has its own colour renderer.
