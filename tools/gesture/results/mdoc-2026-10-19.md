# Round 16, thread D: more device documents (`mdoc`, 2026-10-19)

## Outcome

Six new process devices, each its own optional layer. They read the v2 played score: the event log, the written score and the layers. They are the crossing census, the sympathy trace, the relation ledger, the provenance tree, and two of my own: the score as a staff, and the unheard drawing.

Each one degrades. Without a log, the census reads the crossings off the geometry. The ledger and the staff read the composer's recorded score. Provenance reads the arranger's plan. The sympathy trace and the unheard drawing say, small on the page, that there is nothing to replay.

The two I think are strongest are the staff (image 5) and the unheard drawing (image 6). Both show something only an executable score has: order and silence, and the drawing as it would have been without listening.

Thread A's `crossing` and `sympathy` relations were not available yet. I tested against `cross`, `yield` and a test relation of my own, `mdoc_outcomes`. It is plain on purpose, and its lean is crude. Images 1, 2 and 6 use it, so judge the documents there, not the drawings.

## The catalogue

| # | device | reads | best place |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `mdoc_census` | Log events named gap_under, gap_over, cross, stop or sympathy. Without a log, the geometry: a layer's line that breaks across another layer's line passed under it. **Map** (over): a gap gets the hidden stretch of the under line as a few dashes (the map sign for a line passing beneath); a through-crossing gets a small ring, a stop a bar, a sympathy a tilde. **Table** (beside): one row per pair of layers, every crossing as a pictogram in order, so streaks show as runs. | map over, in an ink the drawing lacks (`ink: auto`); table beside, 90–100 mm |
| 2 | `mdoc_sympathy` | Log events with outcome sympathy. It replays the written score through `synth.run` without that relation and measures how far each played point stands off its unheard course. Short gaps are bridged first, so a break never reads as a lean. **Trace** (over): a hairline beside each stretch that swells by the displacement ×3. **Specimens** (beside): each stretch played over its unheard course (dashes), then the displacement against arc length and its peak in mm. | specimens beside; trace over only when the sheet is light |
| 3 | `mdoc_ledger` | The log aggregated per relation and pair: REL, KIND, WHO HEARD WHOM, HOW (outcomes), N, STEP, WHERE (the frame in miniature with a point per event). A relation whose `p` didn't fire gets SILENT; one that played and met nothing gets NOTHING MET. On v1 it lists the recorded relations with their own notes. Matisse draws the inner rules; the frame and the lettering stay straight (round 11). | beside right, 120 mm |
| 4 | `mdoc_provenance` | Sheet ← layers ← hands (with mm laid) ← source dataset (with sheets or takes), grouped by corpus family. Each hand has its own riser, in its layer's ink. Hands come from the voice channel and voices, a sealed composer's inner score, a v1 composer's score, or the ccolour/arch plan. A hand-less layer hangs from the layer it heard (ECHO, NEST), or says BY THE COMPOSER. | beside right, 110–120 mm |
| 5 | `mdoc_staff` (own) | The score as a score. Time runs left to right, one column per executor step. Each layer is a staff that enters at its step: solid when drawn, double when sealed, dashes when unseen. Each relation is a stem from the heard staff to the acted-on staff, with a ring (contact) or an arrowhead (grow) and the event count. A silent `p` is a rest; a relation that met nothing is a small square. | beside bottom, 50–65 mm |
| 6 | `mdoc_unheard` (own) | The written score replayed with no relations (or without the kinds in `drop`), drawn small beside the real drawing. Every stretch the listening cut, stopped, bent or moved is redrawn in a second ink and ringed, and the header gives the share ("listening changed 88 of 3255 mm (2.7%)"). | beside bottom or right, 110–120 mm |

Shared reader: `kit/devices_mdoc_read.py` holds `played`, `replay` (public executor only), `layers`, `crossing_records` (log or geometry), `under_ends`, `free_ink` and `layer_tag` ("3 MADELIN": drawing order plus name, so equal hands never collide).

Test relation: `kit/relations_mdoc.py` registers `mdoc_outcomes` (contact). It draws one outcome per crossing from weights, with `streak`, and acts it out. Its events carry `s`, `mark` and, for sympathy, `max_mm`.

## Duds and limits

- **The sympathy trace over the drawing** (image 2, left) is the weak reading: a swelling hairline 1.3 mm off the line is easy to lose. The specimens beside it work. I kept the trace as an option.
- **The drawings in images 1, 2 and 6 are test grounds**, not compositions. The sealed listen re-seeds from the score seed, and `mdoc_outcomes`' lean steps visibly. Thread A's relations should be laid under the same documents.
- **The geometry census can't tell a stop from a gap end far from the other line, nor see sympathy.** On listen 15102, the 24 "stops" include lines cut at the mask's edge. That is true geometry, but it isn't a crossing decision.
- **Displacement is a nearest-point distance**, so a lean along the line reads smaller than the relation's own `max_mm` (about 0.3 mm against 0.4–1.1 logged). This is honest about the shape and conservative about the size.
- **The ledger's HOW column on v1 sheets** is the composer's note cut at a word boundary. It is often cut too early ("DRAWN BEFORE THE"). The mini-maps help more than the words.
- The staff is thin on small scores (three layers, three relations). It needs a score with history.

## Honest read against the bar

- **Documents are art here.** I took that at full strength. The staff and the unheard drawing are made to be looked at beside the drawing, not consulted. The staff reads like a musical score of the sheet's making, and Ian's response to the arc diagram suggests he'd keep it. The unheard drawing is the drawing's twin with its awareness ringed. I think it's the most bench-native idea here, and it shows how subtle awareness is: 2.7% of the ink.
- **Variety inside a technique.** The census table is the instrument for that note. It shows whether crossings vary or repeat (runs vs. noise), so it can judge thread A's `streak`.
- **One or two per drawing.** Each image carries one or two documents. Image 6 is at the limit ("too much apparatus" risk), mainly because of the 120 mm panel below.
- **Light over dense sheets.** The census map is light: dashes and 0.75 mm rings in an absent ink. On 15102 it all but disappears at screen scale. That is right for paper, but weak in a review image.
- **Would Ian keep it?** Probably the staff (5), the unheard panel (6) and the provenance tree (4). The census table maybe, once thread A's crossings give it real variety. The ledger is the round-15 ledger's sibling and works, but on v1 sheets it is mostly a table. The sympathy specimens are true but small; their worth depends on thread A's sympathy being worth measuring.

## Proposed core changes (not made)

- The executor's events carry `mark` and `s` on the dst *as it was when the relation ran*. A later relation that cuts or moves the dst shifts those indices. Devices here avoid them (they use `at` and replays). A stable reference would help: the dst mark's id, or `s` measured on the layer's uncut line, as listen's `s0` does.
- `synth.replay(score, drop=…)` as a contract helper would make counterfactual documents a first-class reading. They use `run` on the written score today.
- `apply_devices` "beside" panels stack along one side only. Two beside-right devices sit side by side, widening the sheet each time. A "below the previous panel" option would keep two documents compact.
