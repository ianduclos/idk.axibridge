# Rupture: replacing the scaffold

Ian rejected coexistence as barely changed, still starry and repetitive. This
separate experiment drops its preservation constraint. It replaces every main
course and retains only cropped pieces of the prior `d-grow` texture. No old
base strokes, thorn/surprise, slash/chord, wedge or continuation layers are
included in the new drawing. The old generator still runs to supply the grain;
this is selective reuse of its material, not a claim to have removed that code.

## Construction

The independent scaffold uses a variable number of directional runs, reversals,
straight spans and unequal cubic handles. Complete paths are rotated, scaled
and placed independently of Territory's old course positions. It is still a
procedural family; a different scaffold does not establish unlimited grammar.

Open constructions uses three to five open courses, narrower fragments and
occasional detached grain. Folded bodies uses two to four courses, allows
closure, partial contour removal, broader patches and occasional partial
counter-edges. These are two separate distributions with different random
streams. They do not isolate a single material change on identical curves.
No convergence of the earlier makers or new default has been selected.

Actual grown vertices are cropped and mapped to a finite chord frame of the
new path. A small transverse bend, unequal patch width and shear retain rough
local variations while changing their place and proportions. The source crop
and bed clipping can sever fragments. This does not preserve the original grain
at physical scale, and patches can read as pieces pasted beside a contour.

The first probe mapped grain along the moving path normal. Around corners that
collapsed fibers into fan-like convergences, reintroducing a star-like effect
without calling the thorn routine. Its source and `evidence/probe-v1.png` are
retained. The current frame avoids that particular collapse; pointed grain,
crossings and comb-like repetitions can still occur. No claim of eliminating
every perceived star shape is made.

material from the two new drawings; the earlier reference retains its material.

The earlier reference is independent coexistence on unravelling, not original
Territory. For 13/24 its existing SVG is copied exactly; other seeds regenerate

controls. Python dependencies are in the original checkout's `.venv`.

at about:blank. No local-file browser navigation or substitute server is used.
