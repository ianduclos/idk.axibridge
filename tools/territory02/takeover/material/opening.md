# Opening experiment

Trial 1 is preserved in `frozen-source/opening-trial1.cjs`. Whole-view
inspection of `L-development.png` showed a very dark narrow spindle on 13
and repeated spindle-like bodies on 24: the actual donor grain was compressed
into near-solid marks. The bounded revision opens the flanks more broadly,
retains fewer complete donor strands for air between them, and gives bodies
and individual strands different release timing. The large bare passages
remain part of the experiment.

Up to four long principal courses acquire a local body. Actual `d-grow`
strokes supply its strands, fine deviations, pen lifts, retracing and uneven
ends. Each donor keeps its own start, stop and slight phase difference. The
normal distances compress into the principal course before opening onto
unequal flanks, contract inside the body, open again, then return unevenly.
Intervals are chosen where the real donor material supports a body, using a
bounded coarse search along the course rather than inventing empty openings.
Many course passages and the remaining courses stay bare.

There is no closed silhouette, synthetic hatch fill or repeated parallel
offset family. The principal course remains present through the body; these
are local developments of the approved drawing, not standalone stamps.

Events record the course, physical interval, nominal opening scale,
dominant flank, accepted donor count and fragmentation. `widthMm` is a scale
for a donor 36 mm from its course, not an observed silhouette width. The
`material` stage isolates the added strands for diagnosis.

Limitations: projecting onto a folded course can change which nearby branch
owns a donor point; arc jumps split these strands rather than bridging them.
The earlier ground, wedges and ribbons are absent. Near a body end, dry
donor fragments may converge onto the visible course rather than furnishing
a single continuous outer strand. This is a bounded screen experiment; no
paper acceptance or aesthetic selection is implied.

Verification command: `node tools/territory02/takeover/material/render.cjs
--mode=opening --seeds=13,24,47 --quick`. Repeatability is checked separately
with the same seeds because quick rendering omits it.

The command passed input purity, bed bounds and physical limits on seeds
13, 24 and 47. Separate identical-input calls matched their complete output.
Trial 1 contained 17,309 / 11,649 / 8,704 points and 5,054 / 3,629 / 2,145
mm of ink respectively. The revision contains 9,939 / 8,440 / 5,809 points
and 2,773 / 2,539 / 1,683 mm of ink, with the same checks passing.

The revised three-seed whole view shows air between the strand families on
13 and differentiated release on 24. Some broader material on 24 reads as
detached angular marks, and 47 becomes faint; these remain limitations.
`opening-revision-development.png` records that bounded inspection. The
primary agent retains aesthetic acceptance.
