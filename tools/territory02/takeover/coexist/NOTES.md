# Rough and smooth coexistence

Ian liked the rough three-material studies, sometimes more than the later
smooth grammar probe. His new hand drawing clarified the desired smooth
passages: substantial sweeps, swollen returns, tight throats and abrupt points,
coexisting with strange rough lines and fills. The drawing is a qualitative
reference; no coordinates, colours or individual contours were traced.
The [comparison](index.html) preserves three independent relationships and the
rough input/smooth-only controls. No default production style or convergence
has been selected. [Lead report](../../../../docs/reviews/territory02-coexist-2026-10-05.md).

## Shared input and smooth construction

For each seed, the prior opening, pressure and unravelling generators supply
three separate rough drawings. These are the actual previous outputs, including
their irregular material and inherited devices. We do not rerun them with a
cleaner recipe or globally smooth their strokes. The new smooth constructor
selects up to two real base strokes close to principal Territory courses and
makes an unequal excursion between two points on each selected stroke.

A cubic chain has independently unequal handles and a concentrated tangent
change. Interior stations make broad excursions from the selected interval;
the whole displacement contracts if necessary to remain inside the bed, rather
than clamping points into straight boundary marks. This still is a bounded
four-segment construction, not an unlimited vocabulary. Most reference-image
relationships, colours and proportions are deliberately unspecified by the
algorithm. The smooth-only panel exposes the construction without texture.

## Three independent developments

1. Continuity replaces the real source interval and lets a small selection of
   actual nearby base strokes partly follow the new course. Capture, release,
   occasional renewed capture and short tears differ between donors. Unselected
   strokes survive. Original donor vertices survive subdivision except recorded
   tears/tiny fragments. Their material-versus-bare ownership is inferred from
   an approximate prefix; final clipping in earlier generators prevents a
   guaranteed layer identity. Some bridges become very stretched/angular.
2. Interference replaces the selected interval, measures dense contacts from
   inferred short grain runs, indents the smooth path and displaces nearby grain
   on both sides. Up to four real crossings can split. Original source-index
   exclusions are exact; material classification is inferred and may include
   short displaced bare courses. At most two contacts occur. Opening 13 has no
   qualifying contact: absence is retained rather than filled with a motif.
3. Independent coexistence appends the same smooth paths and keeps every
   original rough stroke byte-identical. The original interval remains too,
   so old and new routes can enclose a space. This is not mechanically identical
   to either replacement experiment and may leave unrelated-looking overlays.

A Sol support implemented continuity; another implemented interference.
The primary owned the smooth constructor, independent coexistence, contracts,
rendering and final judgement. Source ownership stayed separate. A third Sol
reviewer read neutral calibration and population images before explanations.
No source tuning followed the fresh population.

## Evidence and reproduction

Development 13/24 and fresh 1009/1061 each run on all three rough bases: twelve
base cases, five variants each, sixty final drawings. Fresh seeds were first
generated after the source/hash manifest. Neutral labels K/M/N/S/W correspond
to rough/smooth-only/continuity/interference/independent; a/b/c correspond to
opening/pressure/unravelling. Calibration D is continuity 24/unravelling, E is
its source-exchanged stage without moving donors, and F is independent
coexistence 24/unravelling. Whole and close views use the same physical weight.

The fixed shared detail is 100×70 mm around the first smooth path's middle. It
may miss the other smooth path or a material contact. Whole 300×218 mm sheets
are primary evidence. Optional source-exchanged/following-fragments and
smooth-before-contact/mutual-contact SVGs preserve causal stages. The viewer
lets users change both rough base and seed; all cases remain available.

The renderer checks repeatability, unchanged input hashes, finite geometry,
bed bounds, 100 m/250k-point ceilings, stage geometry and exact preservation of
rough strokes in independent coexistence. Physical line width remains 0.1 mm.
These checks establish mechanical constraints, not aesthetic acceptance.
The viewer check exercises 120 image states with own SVG/CSS embedded at
about:blank and no external requests/navigation. Actual in-app/file navigation,
link opening, paper and interactive-response acceptance remain unverified.
The earlier file-URL policy block has not been bypassed.
