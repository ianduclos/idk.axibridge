# Interference — local contact trial

The supplied smooth excursions search the actual surviving material for at most
two weighted line-density contacts. Occupancy excludes the rewritten source
strokes exactly. Remaining material is inferred from runs at most 70 mm long,
excluding strokes longer than 12 mm that lie closely on original bare guides.
This avoids relying on stroke ordering after clipping; it leaves long material
intact but may include some short displaced bare strokes. A dense flank indents the curve
away from that material, with a broad, continuous displacement profile. Only
original grain near the measured curved contact corridor responds: fragments
yield unequally, and up to four existing course/grain crossings split into two
retained halves. No texture is regenerated and no blanket hatch is added.

The function is pure and returns a complete drawing, event measurements and a
before-contact stage. Material classification is geometric inference, not literal
donor-layer provenance. Unchanged material vertices survive
exactly; selected smooth source intervals are replaced through the common
preparation contract. Drawing coordinates use millimetres on a 300 × 218 bed;
the renderer should use the physical 0.1 mm stroke.

Contact is conditional. An excursion missing dense material remains smooth
without an interference event. Boundary-limited displacement is scaled as a
whole, never clamped point by point. The small rigid movement of severed halves
is intentionally modest; whether the reciprocal effect reads clearly at pen
width remains a visual and physical acceptance question for the primary.

Development is restricted to seeds 13 and 24 with opening, pressure and unravel.
Reserved fresh seeds 1009 and 1061 are not used for selection or tuning.

Mechanical development checks covered all six permitted seed/base combinations:
repeat calls match exactly, prepared input stays unchanged, every output vertex
is finite and within the bed, and drawings remain below 51,000 points and 24 m.
Opening/13 produces no contact; opening/24 one; the other four produce two.
Measured curve shifts span 7–14 mm in these runs. Renders and physical legibility
have not been inspected by this implementation agent.
