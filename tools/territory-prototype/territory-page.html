Territory

Each core claims ground for its camp. Borders remember where they were and search again where they were fought over. The machine draws where rival camps meet, where territory runs out, and a few marks inside. Voids are ground nobody may enter. Lines already drawn stay; when the ground shifts, only what moved gets drawn. The plot is the black ink only.
